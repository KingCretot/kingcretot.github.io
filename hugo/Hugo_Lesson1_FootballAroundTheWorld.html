🤔 Let's Think About This

The Big Question

Football is the most popular sport almost everywhere on Earth — except in the United States, where American football, basketball, and baseball come first. Why do you think that is?
Le foot est le sport le plus populaire presque partout sur Terre — sauf aux États-Unis, où le football américain, le basket et le baseball passent en premier. À ton avis, pourquoi?
  • History: the US built its own sports leagues in the 1800s-1900s, before football/soccer became global — home-grown sports got a head start. Les États-Unis ont créé leurs propres ligues avant que le foot ne devienne mondial.
  • Geography & colonization: football spread fastest through the British Empire's old trade and colonial routes — this is why it's huge in Europe, Africa, and South America. Le foot s'est répandu via les anciennes routes de l'Empire britannique.
  • Money & media: American TV networks built massive business models around their home sports, which made switching harder. Les chaînes américaines ont construit d'énormes modèles économiques autour de leurs sports locaux.
