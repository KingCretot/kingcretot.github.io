The 4 Types of Clues

Les 4 types d'indices — apprends à les repérer / learn to spot them
🎓 Detective's Secret

When you don't know a word, the writer almost always leaves you a clue nearby. There are four main types. Once you know them, you'll see them everywhere.

Quand tu ne connais pas un mot, l'auteur laisse presque toujours un indice à côté. Il y a quatre types. Une fois que tu les connais, tu les vois partout.

1
Definition Clue
Indice de définition
The writer defines the word right next to it.
L'auteur définit le mot juste à côté.
The painting was vibrant — full of bright, lively colors.
→ The dash explains "vibrant" = full of bright colors.
2
Example Clue
Indice par exemples
The writer gives examples of the word.
L'auteur donne des exemples du mot.
She loves citrus fruits like lemons, oranges, and grapefruits.
→ The examples tell you "citrus fruits" are sour, juicy fruits.
3
Synonym / Antonym
Synonyme / Contraire
The writer uses a similar or opposite word nearby.
L'auteur utilise un mot similaire ou opposé à côté.
Unlike her quiet and calm sister, Elena was boisterous.
→ "Unlike" + "quiet and calm" = boisterous means the opposite (loud, energetic).
4
Inference Clue
Indice par déduction
No definition given — you think about the situation and figure it out.
Pas de définition — tu réfléchis à la situation et tu devines.
Berenice's stomach was rumbling. She hadn't eaten since lunch. She was famished.
→ Empty stomach + no food = "famished" must mean very hungry.
