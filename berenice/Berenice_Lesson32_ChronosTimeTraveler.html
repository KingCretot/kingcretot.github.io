Lab · Labo

🔬 The Time Layers

📊 Three Layers, One Story

Stories happen at three time layers. Each tense answers a different question about WHEN.
Les histoires se passent en trois couches. Chaque temps répond à une question différente sur QUAND.
PAST SIMPLE "I walked to school." → done, finished, period.
PAST CONTINUOUS "I was walking to school." → in progress at that moment.
PAST PERFECT "I had walked to school by 8am." → done before another past moment.

⚡ When + Past Simple

Two finished actions, one after the other:
"When the bell rang, I jumped."
→ both Past Simple

🔄 While + Past Continuous

Long action interrupted by a quick one:
"While I was eating, the phone rang."
→ Past Continuous + Past Simple

🔑 The Detective Layer (Past Perfect)

When you tell a story and need to mention something that happened EVEN EARLIER, use Past Perfect (had + past participle).

Example: "Berenice was upset because she had forgotten her notebook." → forgetting happened BEFORE being upset.
Quand tu racontes et tu dois mentionner un événement ENCORE PLUS TÔT, utilise Past Perfect (had + participe passé).

Exemple : "Berenice was upset because she had forgotten her notebook." → l'oubli est avant la contrariété.
🇫🇷 Plus-que-parfait flashback: "Elle était triste parce qu'elle avait oublié..." = exactly the same structure! French past tenses are your secret weapon for English ones.
🇫🇷 Le plus-que-parfait : "Elle était triste parce qu'elle avait oublié..." → exactement la même structure ! Les temps français sont ton arme secrète pour l'anglais.

Challenge 3 · Défi 3

🪐 The Master Storyteller

These scenes use multiple tenses together. You'll need to layer them like a real storyteller. Take your time!
Ces scènes utilisent plusieurs temps ensemble. Tu vas devoir les superposer comme une vraie conteuse. Prends ton temps !
Q9
Last summer, Berenice was walking through a park. While walking (long action), she met someone. The person was an old friend she had not seen for years (event from EVEN EARLIER).
L'été dernier, Bérénice se promenait dans un parc. En marchant, elle a croisé une amie qu'elle n'avait pas vue depuis des années.
"While Berenice _____ in the park, she _____ an old friend she _____ for years."
Quels temps choisir ?
Q10
Charline arrived at the airport. At that exact past moment, she realized something. The "forgetting" had happened EARLIER (at home, before leaving). Two layers of past!
Charline est arrivée à l'aéroport. À ce moment passé, elle a réalisé quelque chose. L'oubli était PLUS TÔT (à la maison). Deux couches de passé !
"When Charline arrived at the airport, she realized she _____ her passport at home."
Quel temps choisir ?
Q11
Elena's eyes were red and puffy. The reason: she had been crying for two hours BEFORE that moment. We focus on the duration of the action that came earlier.
Elena avait les yeux rouges. Raison : elle pleurait depuis deux heures AVANT ce moment. On insiste sur la durée.
"Elena's eyes were red because she _____ for two hours."
Quel temps choisir ?
Q12
Berenice realized her mistake — but TOO LATE. Before that moment of realization, she had already written wrong answers (earlier past), and at that moment the test was over (simple past state).
Bérénice a réalisé son erreur — TROP TARD. Avant ce moment, elle avait déjà écrit les mauvaises réponses (passé plus ancien), et à ce moment le test était fini (passé simple).
"By the time Berenice realized her mistake, she _____ the wrong answer on every question and the test _____ over."
Quels temps choisir ?
