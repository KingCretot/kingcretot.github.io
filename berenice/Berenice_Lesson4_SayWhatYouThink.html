Today's Skill · La compétence du jour

🎯 Stating an Opinion — With a Reason

An opinion without a reason is just a feeling. Adding "because" turns it into an argument someone can actually respond to.
Une opinion sans raison n'est qu'un ressenti. Ajouter « because » en fait un argument auquel on peut vraiment répondre.
Stating a view"I think street art is real art."
Pour donner un avis : I think... / In my opinion...
Giving the reason"...because it takes real skill."
Pour justifier : ...because...
Softer opinion"I feel like... / It seems to me that..."
Version plus douce, moins tranchée.
Strong opinion"I'm convinced that... / I firmly believe..."

Meet the Guest · Rencontre avec l'invitée

📋 Meet Today's Guest

Read carefully — notice not just WHAT Sofia believes, but WHY.
Lis attentivement — remarque non seulement CE que Sofia pense, mais POURQUOI.
Guest Bio · Fiche invitée

Meet today's guest: Sofia Marchetti, 15, from Milan. Sofia firmly believes that street art is real art — not vandalism, like some adults in her neighborhood say. Her reason: it takes years of practice to spray-paint a wall in seconds, without any mistakes, before the police arrive. Last year, her older cousin was fined for painting a mural on an abandoned building, and Sofia thinks that was unfair, because the wall was ugly before and beautiful after. She's convinced that cities would be more interesting if more walls looked like that one. Not everyone agrees with her — her own grandmother calls it "just graffiti" — but Sofia has learned to explain her opinion instead of just getting upset about it.
