Lab · Labo

🔬 How Phrasal Verbs Work

🧪 The Formula · La formule

VERB + PARTICLE = NEW MEANING
A "particle" is a small word like up, out, in, on, off, into, down, with. Add it to a normal verb and BOOM — new meaning!
Une "particule" est un petit mot : up, out, in, on, off, into, down, with. Ajoute-la à un verbe normal et BOUM — nouveau sens !

📖 Verb alone · Verbe seul

Just the basic action.
Juste l'action de base.
"I look at the sky." → 👀 actually looking
"I give a gift." → 🎁 actually giving

🎨 Verb + particle · Verbe + particule

Whole new meaning!
Sens totalement nouveau !
"I look up the answer." → 🔍 search for info
"I give up on the puzzle." → 💪 quit trying

🔑 Jungle Strategy · Stratégie de la jungle

When you see a verb + small word and the literal meaning is weird → it's probably a phrasal verb.

Example: "Elena ran into Charline." → Elena didn't crash into her — she met her by chance.
Quand tu vois un verbe + petit mot et que le sens littéral est bizarre → c'est probablement un phrasal verb.

Exemple : "Elena ran into Charline." → Elena ne lui est pas rentrée dedans — elle l'a croisée par hasard.
🇫🇷 Direct French parallel: "Se mettre à" (to start), "tomber sur" (to run into), "donner sur" (to look out onto). French has its own phrasal-verb system — same logic!
🇫🇷 Parallèle direct en français : "Se mettre à", "tomber sur", "donner sur". Le français aussi a ses phrasal verbs — même logique !
