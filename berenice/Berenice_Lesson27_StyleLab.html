Review: Champion's Code 📋

SHOULD / MUST / HAVE TO — encore nets dans ta tête?

📋🏆✅
Champion's Code — SHOULD / MUST / HAVE TO
🇺🇸 What you learned
SHOULD = friendly advice ("You should eat well.")
MUST = strong personal rule / serious obligation ("You must focus.")
HAVE TO = external rule set by someone else ("I have to wear a cap — it's the pool rule.")
🇫🇷 Ce que tu as appris
SHOULD = conseil amical ("Tu devrais manger mieux.")
MUST = obligation forte / règle personnelle ("Tu dois te concentrer.")
HAVE TO = règle extérieure imposée par quelqu'un ("Je dois porter un bonnet — c'est la règle de la piscine.")

🧠 Grammar Focus
The Conditionals 🔀

Two powerful structures — real plans vs. imaginary dreams

Deux structures puissantes — projets réels vs. rêves imaginaires

💡 Good News for You!
French has the exact same two conditionals! "Si + présent → futur" and "Si + imparfait → conditionnel." English works the same way — just different words! 🎉
💡 Bonne nouvelle pour toi!
Le français a exactement les mêmes deux conditionnels! "Si + présent → futur" et "Si + imparfait → conditionnel." L'anglais fonctionne de la même façon — juste avec des mots différents!
🔀 First vs. Second Conditional — Side by Side
FIRST
1er conditionnel
IF + present simple → WILL + base verb
"If I go to Paris Fashion Week, I will take a thousand photos." → REAL possibility!
"Si je vais à la Fashion Week de Paris, je prendrai mille photos."
"If it rains this weekend, I will stay home and watch films." → Could happen!
"S'il pleut ce week-end, je resterai chez moi regarder des films."
SECOND
2ème conditionnel
IF + past simple → WOULD + base verb
"If I could design clothes, I would create my own brand." → IMAGINARY dream!
"Si je pouvais créer des vêtements, je créerais ma propre marque."
"If I had a million euros, I would open a boutique in Paris." → Not real now!
"Si j'avais un million d'euros, j'ouvrirais une boutique à Paris."
✅ FIRST CONDITIONAL
IF + present → WILL
"If the shop is open, I will go tomorrow."
Situation réelle et possible — ça pourrait vraiment arriver!
💜 SECOND CONDITIONAL
IF + past → WOULD
"If I were a designer, I would live in Milan."
Situation imaginaire ou peu probable — ce n'est pas réel maintenant!
⚠️ Critical Rule — Règle critique!
NEVER say "If I would..." — After IF, NEVER use WOULD! ❌
❌ Wrong: "If I would go to Paris, I will be happy."
✅ Right: "If I go to Paris, I will be happy." (1st conditional)
✅ Right: "If I went to Paris, I would be happy." (2nd conditional)
Ne jamais dire "If I would..." — Après IF, JAMAIS de WOULD! Utilise le présent (1er) ou le passé (2ème conditionnel).
⚡ Grammar Practice
Conditional Workout 💪

First or Second? WILL or WOULD? Let's find out!

Premier ou Deuxième? WILL ou WOULD? C'est parti!

🎯 How to Choose
First Conditional — Ask yourself: "Could this actually happen?" If YES → use present + WILL.
Second Conditional — Ask yourself: "Is this imaginary or very unlikely?" If YES → use past + WOULD.
🎯 Comment choisir
1er Conditionnel — Demande-toi : "Est-ce que ça pourrait vraiment arriver?" Si OUI → présent + WILL.
2ème Conditionnel — Demande-toi : "C'est imaginaire ou très peu probable?" Si OUI → passé + WOULD.

Léa's Big Dream

Léa is 14 years old and lives in Paris. She loves fashion more than anything else. Every morning, she picks her outfit carefully — she always looks for something unique and bold. Her style is inspired by vintage films and Japanese street culture.

This year, Léa entered a school design contest. She spent three months creating her collection — eight looks, each with perfectly chosen accessories that match the main piece. Her designs were incredible. Her best piece? A white jacket with a bright red collar — a real statement that stopped everyone in the room.

"If I win the contest," she told her friends excitedly, "I will display my collection at the city youth festival in June!" She already knew exactly what she would wear that day.

"If I could choose any designer job in the world," she said to her mother, "I would work for a magazine and follow every new trend in the world."

Her friends say she has real talent. Her mother agrees. But Léa knows one thing for sure: if she works hard and stays consistent, she will find her path — one beautiful design at a time.
