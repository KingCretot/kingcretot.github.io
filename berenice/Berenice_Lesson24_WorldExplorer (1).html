🎌 What are Japanese teenagers doing?
📱
Gaming & Anime
Japanese teens love anime, manga, and video games. Akihabara in Tokyo is a whole neighborhood dedicated to gaming culture — called the Electric Town!
🌸
Hanami Festivals
Every spring, families and teenagers go to parks for hanami — picnics under the cherry blossom trees. One of Japan's most beloved traditions!
🍱
School Lunch Culture
In Japan, students serve each other school lunch in the classroom — a daily tradition of community and respect called kyushoku!
👘
Wearing Yukata
During festivals, many teens wear a yukata — a light summer kimono. This colorful clothing connects modern youth to centuries of tradition.

🇲🇦 What are Moroccan teenagers doing?
🍵
Mint Tea Ritual
Serving mint tea to guests is a sacred Moroccan hospitality ritual. Teenagers learn to pour tea from a great height to create foam — it is a true art!
🥁
Gnawa Music
Gnawa is a hypnotic Moroccan style mixing African rhythms with Arabic influences. Young Moroccans today mix it with hip-hop and electronic music!
⭐
Ramadan & Community
During Ramadan, families gather each evening to break fast together. For Moroccan teens, it is a month of community, incredible food, prayer, and celebration.
🏺
Arts & Crafts
Moroccan teens are surrounded by stunning traditional crafts — mosaic tiles (zellige), colorful pottery, and hand-woven carpets. Many learn these crafts from family!

🇧🇷 What are Brazilian teenagers doing?
⚽
Football Culture
Football is more than a sport in Brazil — it is a religion! Teens play futebol on streets, beaches, and in school. Brazil has won the FIFA World Cup 5 times!
🎵
Funk Carioca
Brazil's most popular teen music is Funk Carioca, born in Rio's favelas. It mixes electronic beats with powerful city-life lyrics. Artists like Anitta are global stars!
🏖️
Açaí Bowl Culture
After swimming at the beach, Brazilian teens eat açaí bowls — a purple superfruit from the Amazon topped with granola and banana. A daily ritual!
💃
Carnival & Samba
Brazilian teens spend months rehearsing samba for Carnival — the world's biggest street party! Each city neighborhood competes with its own spectacular parade.

Diwali — Festival of Lights
Diwali is the biggest Indian celebration! Teens decorate homes with oil lamps (diyas), light fireworks, and share sweets. The Festival of Lights lasts 5 whole days!
🎬

🏏

Cricket is India's national sport — players are treated like superstars! Yoga, invented in India over 5,000 years ago, is practiced by millions of Indian teens every morning.

🇵🇪 What are Peruvian teenagers doing?
🏔️
Inca Heritage & Pride
Peruvian teens are incredibly proud of Inca heritage. At school, they study Quechua — the ancient Inca language — and many still speak it at home today!
🍋
World's Best Cuisine?
Peru has been voted the world's number one gastronomy destination multiple times! Teens eat ceviche (fish in lime juice), causa (layered potato dish), and picarones (sweet doughnuts)!
🎊
Inti Raymi — Sun Festival
Inti Raymi (Festival of the Sun) is celebrated every June in Cusco. Thousands dress in traditional Inca costumes and re-enact ancient ceremonies. Breathtaking to see!
🦙
Alpacas & Andean Life
In the Andes mountains, many Peruvian teens grow up alongside alpacas and llamas. Alpaca wool is woven into the colorful textiles Peru is famous for around the world!

Last summer, I traveled to three incredible countries. First, I flew to Tokyo, Japan, where I visited ancient temples and tried ramen for the first time. The food was absolutely breathtaking — the cuisine blew my mind!


Next, I went to Morocco, where I explored the colorful souks of Marrakech. The ancient medina took my breath away. I had mint tea with a Moroccan family — it was a stunning experience.


Today, I believe that travel is the greatest education. The world has so many diverse cultures, traditions, and cuisines. Every country I visit teaches me something new.


Next year, I will travel to Brazil for Carnival, and I will explore the Amazon rainforest. I will also visit Peru to see Machu Picchu — I know the view will be breathtaking! — Clara 🌍
