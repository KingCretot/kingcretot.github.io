How to Pay with Step Up For Students

KingCretot Experience is an approved Step Up For Students provider. Through Step Up, Zoila’s program is 23 hours at $65 per hour = $1,495.00 for the full school year. You can submit all 23 hours at once, or month by month using the schedule below.

How to request KCE in Step Up:

  1. Log in to your EMA account at stepupforstudents.org.
  2. Go to the Marketplace and open Provider Search.
  3. Search for “KingCretot Experience, LLC” (or “Jonathan King-Cretot”).
  4. Check how much Zoila has available for tutoring.
  5. Submit the request at $65.00 per hour: 23 hours in total ($1,495.00), all at once or month by month.
  6. Once you submit, the request comes to us for review and approval. We can’t process anything on our end until this step is done.
