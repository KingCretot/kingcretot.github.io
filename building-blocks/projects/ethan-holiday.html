🏅 The Ferguson Family Winter Games

Thursday, December 17 – Tuesday, January 5 · Ethan presents it at his first January lesson, Wednesday, January 6 (in person)

What it is: Ethan is the Games Director of a family Winter Games with three events: Paper Airplane Distance ✈️, Paper Airplane Hang Time, and the Sock Toss. Everyone plays. Ethan measures and records the data, makes a bar graph, turns places into points (adding and multiplying), compares decimals and fractions, collects quotes, and writes a news report. Every event starts and ends with a bow or a handshake, the way it is done on the jiu-jitsu mat. It all goes into a stapled Winter Games Record Book that Ethan presents in 3 to 5 minutes on January 6.

Time: 10 to 25 minutes a day for Ethan, shown in blue. Christmas Eve, Christmas Day and New Year's Day are off.

Supplies

  • The printed worksheets (use the Print the worksheets button on this page): 14 pages, printed once
  • Plain paper for paper airplanes (a few sheets for each person)
  • A tape measure, and masking tape or painter's tape for a start line
  • A phone or watch with a stopwatch
  • A few pairs of rolled-up socks and a laundry basket
  • Crayons or markers, scissors, a stapler, and ribbon or string for the medals

The Respect Rules (Ethan reads these out loud on Day 1)

  1. Bow or shake hands before and after every event.
  2. Cheer for everybody, not only the winner.
  3. Little-kid rule: grown-ups and Ethan stand behind the start line; Joyce and Clyde start closer. Ethan decides how much closer.
  4. Measure carefully and write every number down exactly.
  5. The Head Judge (Dad) has the final say.
