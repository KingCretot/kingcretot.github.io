📰 The Simon Family Holiday Gazette

Sunday, December 20 – Sunday, January 3 · The girls present it at Lesson 19 on Monday, January 4 (Zoom)

What it is: The family makes its own holiday newspaper. Bailey is the Editor. Bristol is the Reporter and Pet Columnist. Mom and Dad are interviewed, the family bakes together, River and Cam get their own diary page, everyone does kind acts and sets a goal for 2027, and it all goes into one gazette the girls show on Zoom on January 4.

Time: about 20–30 minutes on work days. Christmas Eve, Christmas Day and the day after are off except for the Kindness Log. New Year's Eve and New Year's Day are fully off.

Supplies

  • The printed worksheets (use the Print the worksheets button on the project page)
  • Pencils, markers or crayons, glue or tape, a stapler
  • Ingredients for one family recipe you choose together. Pick one that goes on a tray in rows (cookies, muffins or rolls), so Bristol can do Tray Math.
  • A photo of River and Cam (printed), or the girls draw them

The gazette, page by page

  1. Front Page: "Our Family Tradition" story (Bailey writes it, Bristol draws the picture and writes the caption)
  2. Kitchen Corner: the family recipe and the math from baking it
  3. The Pet Page: diaries written by River (Bristol) and Cam (Bailey)
  4. Kindness Report: the family's kind acts, counted
  5. Hello, 2027: everyone's goals for the new year

anticipate: to look forward to something before it happens
reminisce: to talk or think about happy times from the past
resolution: a promise to yourself to do something better in the new year
jubilant: very, very happy, like you want to cheer
