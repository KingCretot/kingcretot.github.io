Teacher Plan · Lesson 3: Regrouping and Theo's Camping Trip

Tuesday, November 10, 2026 · In person · 60 minutes · Week 3: Regrouping and Sequencing · Focus: Adding and subtracting with regrouping + sequencing and story structure (Pathways Week 3)

🎯 Objective: Math (28 min): Ce'Line adds 2- and 3-digit numbers with regrouping (carrying once and twice, ones first, right to left) and subtracts with regrouping (borrowing once and twice, and across a zero, as in 405 − 168), checks a subtraction by adding back, and finds the two classic mistakes (flipping 2 − 7 to 7 − 2, and forgetting the carried 1). Reading (18 min): she reads Theo's overnight camping story from Pathways Week 3 Reading, tells what the beginning, middle and end do, finds the transition words first, next, then and last, and puts the events in sequence. This is everything on the Pathways Week 3 Math and Reading retakes.
Student goal: I can add and subtract with regrouping. I can tell what happens first, next, then and last in a story.

Watch for

  • Flipping to subtract: in 362 − 147 she may do 7 − 2 = 5 in the ones and get 225. Say: "The top number is what we have. Is 2 big enough to take away 7? No, so we borrow."
  • Forgetting the carried 1: 259 + 168 = 417 instead of 427. Have her write the 1 in red at the top of the next column before she adds that column.
  • Writing 15 in one place: 3615-type answers mean she wrote the whole sum in the ones place. Go back to the pieces: only 0 to 9 fit in one place.
  • Not lowering the lender: 352 − 127 = 235 means she borrowed but did not change the 5 tens to 4. Every borrow is two marks: cross out and write one less, then add 10 to the ones.
  • Across a zero: for 405 − 168 or 300 − 146, the 0 tens become 10 and then 9. 247 or 164 means she left the tens at 10. Pathways Week 3 Math has this in the Boss Battle ($500 − $235).
  • Pathways Week 3 Math typed answers must be exact: it accepts only regroup (not regrouping), borrow, carry, sum, difference, ones (start column), ten (10 ones are traded for 1 what?), right to left, 1 less (what happens to the tens digit when it lends) and yes / no. Tell her before she starts.
  • Pathways Week 3 Reading typed answers must be exact too: the three transition words in the middle are typed first, next, then; the beginning sets up "who is in it and what is about to happen" (it wants the whole phrase "who is in it and what is about to happen"); a good ending wraps up the feeling; Theo feels grateful; and "words like first, next, then and last are called ___ words" is transition. Names are typed as one word: mia, dad, stream, stones, chili, map, overnight, stars.
  • Reading support: she scored 56 on Pathways Week 1 Reading. Read the Theo passages with her (you read a sentence, she reads a sentence) and point back to the exact sentence for every answer. Words to pre-teach: overnight, grinned, clearing, pitched, crackled, compete.
