Week 3

Regrouping and Sequencing

Focus: Adding and subtracting with regrouping + sequencing and story structure (Pathways Week 3)
Tue, Nov 10 · 🏫 In person
Lesson 3 · Pathways Week 3

Regrouping and Theo's Camping Trip

🎯 I can add and subtract with regrouping. I can tell what happens first, next, then and last in a story.

Ce'Line learns to regroup: she carries when a column adds up to 10 or more, and she borrows when the top digit is too small to subtract, one column at a time, ones first (even across a zero, like 405 − 168). Then she follows Theo's overnight camping trip and puts the story in sequence: beginning, middle and end, and the transition words first, next, then and last. This gets her ready for the Pathways Week 3 Math and Reading retakes.
