The Runaway Kite
Kofi and his dad went to the beach on a windy afternoon. Kofi held the string of his new green kite. A big gust pulled the string out of his hand! The kite flew down the beach. Kofi did not cry. He ran across the hot sand. He jumped over a sand castle. He grabbed the string just in time. "Got it!" he yelled with a big smile.
