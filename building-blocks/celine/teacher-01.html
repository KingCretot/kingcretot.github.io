Watch for

  • Digit vs. value: asked for the value of the 6 in 362, she may type 6. Ask: which place is it in? What is one ten strip worth? Have her say the full sentence: "The 6 is in the tens place, so it is worth 60."
  • The 0 place holder: 500 + 0 + 2 written as 52, or 407 expanded as 400 + 7. Point to the empty column on the mat: the 0 holds the place. Pathways tests this in its Boss Battle (numbers like 306 and 504).
  • Rounding to a 5: typing 65 or 85 for "round 64 to the nearest ten." Rounding always lands on a ten (or a hundred). Have her name the two tens first.
  • Looking at the wrong digit: for the nearest hundred she must look at the tens digit, not the ones. If 437 becomes 440, she rounded to the nearest ten. Cover the ones digit with a finger.
  • The 5 rule: 35, 25, 450 and 650 all go up. If she rounds them down, repeat: 5 or more, go up.
  • Setting = where AND when: she may name only the place ("the woods"). Ask: what time of day? Also, things a character carries (Theo's compass, Jamal's flashlight) are not the setting.
  • Infer from the text, not from a guess: every trait answer needs a clue she can point to. Ask: which words told you that?
  • Confidence: her scores so far are low and she needs a lot of support. Praise every correct step out loud, keep your voice calm on wrong answers, and let her look back at the slide or the story before she answers.
