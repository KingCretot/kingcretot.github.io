Teacher Plan · Lesson 16: Spelling Teams: ea

Monday, December 14, 2026 · Zoom · 60 minutes · Week 7: Spelling Teams: ea and oo · Focus: Spelling patterns (ea, oo)

🎯 Objective: Bristol learns that a vowel team is two vowels that make one sound, and that ea usually says long e (eat, read, beach, clean, dream, team). She learns that a few ea words say short e (head, bread) and reads new ea words by trying long e first. She tells long e (ea) from short e (e) in pairs like red / read and net / neat, which fixes her red-for-read spelling. Week 7 Day 1 (Learn it).
Student goal: I can spell long e words with the ea team, like read and beach.
Mastery to unlock the next lesson: 85

Watch for

  • red for read (her own mistake). Ask: "Is it a color, or something you do with a book?"
  • Writing only e for a long e sound (bech, clen, lef). Ask: "Long e or short e? Long e needs the team."
  • Writing the team backward (ae: baech, claen). The e comes first, and the e does the talking.
  • Reading head or bread with long e. Remind her: try long e first; if it does not make sense, try short e.
  • Writing ee for an ea word (meet for meat, reed for read). Both teams say long e, so this is a memory word. Say which word it is (meet a friend vs. meat to eat). We do not test ee this week.
  • ea words with r (year, near, learn) or long a (great, break) if she finds them in her book. Tell her they are different and we learn them later.
