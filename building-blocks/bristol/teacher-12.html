Teacher Plan · Lesson 12: Welcome Back: What Do You Remember?

Thursday, December 3, 2026 · In person · 60 minutes · Week 5: Welcome Back Review · Focus: Retention / spaced review

🎯 Objective: Bristol presents her Gratitude Project book, then shows what she kept from Weeks 1–4 after the three-week break: equal groups → multiply and not equal → add; the fact tricks for 4, 6, 7, 8 and 9 (mixed, with missing factors); the 3 detective questions; and price-tag problems (total, how many can I buy, who spends more). Week 5 (Review). No new skill. The goal is to find out what she kept and what slipped.
Student goal: I can show what I remember about groups, facts and word problems.
Mastery to unlock the next lesson: 85

Materials

  • The Class lesson open on your laptop (course page, code 9110)
  • Her Thankful Book and her Count the Slips page (page 3 of the Gratitude Project)
  • About 40 beans and 6 paper cups
  • Play money: about 30 $1 bills and 8 $5 bills
  • 5 index cards for her trick cards
  • Whiteboard and marker
  • Her book, Amy and the Missing Puppy
  • Printed packet (2 pages) to send home

60-minute plan

0:00–0:02Hello
Class slide 1. Book and page 3 on the table.
0:02–0:10Show and Tell: My Thankful Book
Class slide 2. She reads her book out loud and shows page 3. You are the audience. Praise one true thing.
0:10–0:14Your slips are math
Class slide 3. 6 piles of 5 → 6 × 5 = 30. River's pile of 4 → not equal → add (29).
0:14–0:19Book Talk
Class slide 4. How the book ended, whether her guess from Lesson 11 was right, what Amy learned.
0:19–0:23Brain check
Class slide 5. She tells everything she remembers with no help. You check it off on the whiteboard.
0:23–0:29Equal or not + sort
Class slides 6–7. Beans in cups, then the sort. She says equal or not equal before each tap.
0:29–0:38Fact tricks
Class slides 8–9. She makes 5 trick cards, then solves 4 facts, saying the trick first.
0:38–0:42Missing factors
Class slide 10. Skip count and count the jumps.
0:42–0:47Detective questions
Class slides 11–12. The 3 questions, then she names the operation for 3 stories.
0:47–0:54Price tags
Class slides 13–15. Pay with play money, build 7 groups of $4, then 3 shop problems.
0:54–0:57Be the teacher
Class slide 16. She names each mistake before she picks.
0:57–1:00Wrap-up
Class slide 17. What was easy, what to practice. Show the task list.

Watch for

  • Facts that slipped over the break, most likely 7s and 8s (54 for 7 × 8, 48 for 6 × 7). Point to her trick card and have her say the trick before the answer.
  • Multiplying when the groups are not equal (6 × 5 for River's pile of 4). Have her count each group out loud.
  • Giving the last skip-count number instead of the number of jumps ("80" for how many puzzles). Ask: "Is 80 puzzles or dollars?"
  • Number grabbing in word problems: adding 4 + 7 when each bag has 7. Ask the 3 detective questions.
  • Regrouping slips in subtraction (42 − 17 as 35). Have her add back to check.
  • Mixing up the price (one) and the total (all).
