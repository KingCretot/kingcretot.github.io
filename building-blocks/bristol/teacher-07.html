Teacher Plan · Lesson 7: Sevens: 5 and 2 More

Monday, November 9, 2026 · Zoom · 60 minutes · Week 3: Facts for 7, 8 and 9 · Focus: Facts 4, 6, 7, 8, 9

🎯 Objective: Bristol finds any 7s fact by splitting 7 into 5 and 2: she finds the 5s part and the 2s part (both facts she owns) and adds them (7 × 6 = 30 + 12 = 42). She sees the split in real arrays and in a calendar, and she keeps practicing last Tuesday's 8s and 9s tricks.
Student goal: I can split 7 into 5 and 2 to find my 7s facts.
Mastery to unlock the next lesson: 85

Materials

  • Zoom with screen share (open the Class lesson; teacher code)
  • At her house, next to her (ask Tia to set them out before class): about 70 beans or counters in two colors (or plain beans and a crayon to mark), a ruler or long paper strip, and a calendar page
  • Your whiteboard and marker, on camera
  • Index cards you hold up to the camera: one 7s fact on each (7 × 3 to 7 × 9)
  • Her book, Amy and the Missing Puppy (next to her)
  • She holds up the Lesson 5 packet and Lesson 6 packet page 1 to the camera (collect them on Tuesday). Lesson 7 packet (2 pages): hand it out at Lesson 8 on Tuesday

60-minute plan

0:00–0:03Hello
Class slide 1. Share your screen. She holds up the Lesson 5 packet and Lesson 6 packet page 1 to the camera. Look at last Tuesday's Show What You Know score. She says the 8s and 9s tricks.
0:03–0:10Book Talk
Class slide 2. She retells in order with First, Next, Then. If she jumps around, ask: what happened before that?
0:10–0:15Warm-up: 8s, 9s, 6s
Class slide 3. Before each answer, she names the trick. She says the answer; you tap it.
0:15–0:27Split the 7 (hands-on)
Class slides 4–6. At home, on camera, she builds 7 rows of 4 beans with the ruler under row 5; she says the top part, bottom part and total and you type them (slide 5). Then 7 rows of 6 beans for slide 6.
0:27–0:35Build and try
Class slides 7–8. Bean piles at home for the puppy toys (she tells you how to set the + buttons), then three taps: she says the answer, you tap. Say 5, 6, 7, 8 together.
0:35–0:41Split the right number
Class slides 9–10. She lays the ruler across the rows of 7 × 6 on camera: why 49 is wrong. Then she says the 7 × 9 steps in order while you tap them.
0:41–0:47Calendar array
Class slides 11–12. She counts 4 weeks on her calendar page by rows, then says the days in 10 weeks and 9 weeks while you type.
0:47–0:53Check the split and card flip
Class slides 13–14. Write each split on your whiteboard on camera; she checks each part out loud. Then the 7s index cards, held up to the camera: 5s part, 2s part, answer. Slow cards go aside for Tuesday.
0:53–0:57Word problems
Class slide 15. She reads aloud and says equal groups or not before she tells you what to tap.
0:57–1:00Wrap-up
Class slide 16. Read the task list together: tonight the after-class lesson (with Show What You Know) and 10 minutes of reading; Tuesday night the Lesson 7 Practice and Lesson 6 packet page 2; Wednesday both pages of the Lesson 7 packet (the Week 12 reading lesson moved to break week 2 on the Lesson 9 list). The Lesson 7 packet goes home with her on Tuesday.

Watch for

  • Using 7 in both parts: 7 × 6 as 5 × 7 + 2 × 7 (= 49, which is 7 × 7). Ask: which number tells the rows? Which tells how many in each row?
  • Writing the 2s part as just 2 (7 × 3 = 15 + 2). Point to the 2 rows: how many beans in each row?
  • 7 × 8 = 54. Say the pattern together: 5, 6, 7, 8 (56 = 7 × 8).
  • Answering 49 in the ribbon problem (7 ribbons and 7 more). She multiplied because both numbers are 7. Ask: are there 7 equal groups, or just two amounts?
