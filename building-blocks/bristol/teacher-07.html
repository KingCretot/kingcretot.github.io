Teacher Plan · Lesson 7: Sevens: 5 and 2 More

Tuesday, November 3, 2026 · In person · 60 minutes · Week 3: Facts for 7, 8 and 9 · Focus: Facts 4, 6, 7, 8, 9

🎯 Objective: Bristol finds any 7s fact by splitting 7 into 5 and 2: she finds the 5s part and the 2s part (both facts she owns) and adds them (7 × 6 = 30 + 12 = 42). She sees the split in real arrays and in a calendar, and she keeps practicing yesterday's 8s and 9s tricks.
Student goal: I can split 7 into 5 and 2 to find my 7s facts.
Mastery to unlock the next lesson: 85

Materials

  • About 70 beans or counters in two colors (or plain beans and a crayon to mark)
  • A long strip of paper or a ruler to split an array
  • A real calendar page
  • Whiteboard and marker
  • Index cards: one 7s fact on each (7 × 3 to 7 × 9)
  • Her book, Amy and the Missing Puppy
  • Packet (2 pages) to send home

60-minute plan

0:00–0:07Book Talk
Slide 2. She retells in order with First, Next, Then. If she jumps around, ask: what happened before that?
0:07–0:13Warm-up: 8s, 9s, 6s
Slide 3. Check last night's packet quickly. Before each tap, she names the trick: double the 4s, 10s take away a group, 5s plus a group.
0:13–0:28Split the 7 (hands-on)
Before slide 4, she builds 7 rows of 4 beans. Lay the ruler under row 5. Ask: how many in the top part? The bottom part? Together? Then slides 4–6. Repeat with 7 rows of 6 on the table.
0:28–0:38Practice the split
Slides 7–9. On the calendar page, she counts the days in 4 weeks by rows. On slide 9 she says each step aloud before tapping.
0:38–0:46Check the split
Slide 10. For each card she checks both parts on the whiteboard. Then flip the index cards: she says the 5s part, the 2s part and the answer.
0:46–0:51Word problems
Slide 11. She reads aloud and says equal groups or not before she picks.
0:51–0:57Show What You Know
Slide 12, on her own. She needs 85 (7 of 8) to open Lesson 8.
0:57–1:00Wrap-up
Show the task list, including Times Table Trail Lesson 4. Packet pages and the practice are due Thursday.

Watch for

  • Splitting the wrong number: 7 × 6 as 5 × 7 + 2 × 7. Ask: which number tells the rows? Which tells how many in each row?
  • Writing the 2s part as just 2 (7 × 3 = 15 + 2). Point to the 2 rows: how many beans in each row?
  • 7 × 8 = 54. Say the pattern together: 5, 6, 7, 8 (56 = 7 × 8).
  • Answering 49 in the ribbon problem (7 ribbons and 7 more). She multiplied because both numbers are 7. Ask: are there 7 equal groups, or just two amounts?

Linked KCE lessons

  • Times Table Trail, Lesson 4: Multiplying by 6, 7, 8 and 9: Do the whole lesson. For 7s, say 5s part + 2s part. For 8s, double the 4s. For 9s, 10s take away one group. If it is locked, type 1111.

If a linked lesson is locked, the student types 1111 (you get a Formspree note), or you type 9110.
