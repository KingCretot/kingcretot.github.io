Teacher Plan · Lesson 14: Contraction Families

Tuesday, December 8, 2026 · In person · 60 minutes · Week 6: Contractions · Focus: Contractions

🎯 Objective: Bristol makes and takes apart the pronoun contraction families: I'm; you're, we're, they're; he's, she's, it's; I'll, you'll, we'll; I've, we've, they've. She learns to look after the apostrophe to find the hiding word (s → is, re → are, ll → will, ve → have) and fixes apostrophes in the wrong spot (did'nt → didn't). Week 6 Day 2 (Practice it).
Student goal: I can make contractions like I'm, they're, she's, I'll and we've.
Mastery to unlock the next lesson: 85

Watch for

  • Keeping the dropped letter and adding an apostrophe (they'are, I'am). Ask: "Which letter drops out?"
  • Apostrophe in the wrong spot (the'yre, theyr'e, did'nt). Ask: "Where was the missing letter? Put it right there."
  • your for you're, and were for we're. Say the long way: "you are my friend" makes sense, so it's you're.
  • I'll written as Ill or I'l. Two l's stay; the apostrophe replaces w and i.
  • A lowercase i in I'm, I'll and I've. I is always a capital letter.
  • it's and its. If she asks: it's with an apostrophe is short for it is. Its with no apostrophe shows something belongs to it (we learn that later).
  • Typed answers ignore apostrophes, so check the spot on her whiteboard and in the packet.
