Teacher Plan · Lesson 4: Sixes: Fives and One More

Tuesday, October 27, 2026 · In person · 60 minutes · Week 2: Facts for 4 and 6 · Focus: Facts 4, 6, 7, 8, 9

🎯 Objective: Bristol learns ×6 as the 5s fact plus one more group (6 × 7 = 35 + 7 = 42), using the 5s she already knows. She then mixes 4s (double, double from Lesson 3) and 6s, picks the right trick for each fact (including turn-arounds like 9 × 6), and keeps choosing multiply or add in word problems.
Student goal: I can find 6 times a number with my 5s and one more group.
Mastery to unlock the next lesson: 85

Materials

  • About 60 counters or dried beans
  • 6 small cups or paper plates
  • An index card or sticky note (to cover one group or one row)
  • Whiteboard and marker
  • Her book, Amy and the Missing Puppy
  • Printed packet (2 pages) to send home

60-minute plan

0:00–0:07Book Talk
Slide 2. Ask who helps Amy the most and how. Ask for one clue from the book and what she would do as Amy.
0:07–0:12Remember: double, double
Slide 3. Before she taps, she says the two doubles out loud ("7, 14, 28"). Check yesterday's practice score and packet; fix any 4s fact she missed.
0:12–0:245 groups + 1 more (hands-on)
Make 6 cups of 4 beans. Cover one cup with the index card: 5 × 4 = 20. Uncover it: 20 + 4 = 24. Repeat with 6 cups of 7. Then slides 4–5; on the array, she covers the bottom row with the card.
0:24–0:34Practice the 6s
Slides 6–8. She builds 6 bowls of 6 with beans first, then on the screen. For each fact, she says the 5s fact first, then "plus one more ___".
0:34–0:44Mix 4s and 6s
Slides 9–10. In the sort, she must say the trick and the answer before tapping. For 8 × 4 and 9 × 6, ask: "Turn it around. Which number is 4 or 6?"
0:44–0:50Word problems
Slide 11. She reads each one aloud and points to the word each (or says it is missing) before she taps.
0:50–0:57Show What You Know
Slide 12, on her own. No hints. She needs 85 (7 of 8) to open Lesson 5.
0:57–1:00Wrap-up
Show the task list. Thursday is Prove it: mixed 4s and 6s, missing numbers and word problems.

Watch for

  • Adding 1 instead of one more group (6 × 7 = 36). Point to the covered cup: "How many beans are under the card?"
  • Adding the wrong group size (6 × 8 = 40 + 6). Ask: "How many are in each group?"
  • Using the 6s trick on a 4s fact or the other way around. Have her circle the 4 or the 6 first.
  • Regrouping slips in 45 + 9 and 35 + 7. Let her write it in tens and ones on the whiteboard.
