Teacher Plan · Lesson 4: Sixes: Fives and One More

Tuesday, October 27, 2026 · In person · 60 minutes · Week 2: Facts for 4 and 6 · Focus: Facts 4, 6, 7, 8, 9

🎯 Objective: Bristol learns ×6 as the 5s fact plus one more group (6 × 7 = 35 + 7 = 42), using the 5s she already knows. She then mixes 4s (double, double from Lesson 3) and 6s, picks the right trick for each fact (including turn-arounds like 9 × 6), and keeps choosing multiply or add in word problems.
Student goal: I can find 6 times a number with my 5s and one more group.
Mastery to unlock the next lesson: 85

Materials

  • About 60 counters or dried beans
  • 6 small cups or paper plates
  • An index card or sticky note (to cover one group or one row)
  • Whiteboard and marker
  • Her book, Amy and the Missing Puppy
  • Printed Lesson 3 and Lesson 4 packets to send home if you can (her task list also tells her to print them)

60-minute plan

0:00–0:08Hello and Book Talk
Class slides 1–2. State the goal. Who helps Amy, one clue, what she would do next.
0:08–0:12Remember: double, double
Class slide 3. She says the two doubles out loud before answering. Check last night's after-class score and fix any 4s fact she missed.
0:12–0:245 groups + 1 more (cups and card)
Class slides 4–8. Quick 5s round. 6 cups of 4 beans, cover one cup: 20 + 4. Then 6 × 3 with cups, 6 × 7 on the whiteboard array, and Theo's 35 + 1 mistake with the card over a cup of 7.
0:24–0:33Practice the 6s
Class slides 9–11. She says the 5s fact, then "plus one more ___". She builds 6 cups of 6 with beans before the screen. Order the steps for 6 × 9.
0:33–0:44Mix 4s and 6s
Class slides 12–14. Circle the 4 or the 6 on the whiteboard, then the sort (trick and answer before each tap), then read the pictures.
0:44–0:50Word problems
Class slide 15. She reads each one aloud and points to each or says it is missing.
0:50–0:56Fact race
Class slide 16. She writes the trick work for 5 facts on the whiteboard before you click.
0:56–1:00Wrap-up
Class slide 17. Read the task list together: Tuesday night and Wednesday items. It includes Lesson 3's practice and packet.

Watch for

  • Adding 1 instead of one more group (6 × 7 = 36). Point to the covered cup: "How many beans are under the card?"
  • Adding the wrong group size (6 × 8 = 40 + 6). Ask: "How many are in each group?"
  • Using the 6s trick on a 4s fact or the other way around. Have her circle the 4 or the 6 first.
  • Regrouping slips in 45 + 9 and 35 + 7. Let her write it in tens and ones on the whiteboard.
