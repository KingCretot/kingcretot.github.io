Teacher Plan · Lesson 8: Prove It: Mixed Facts

Thursday, November 5, 2026 · In person · 60 minutes · Week 3: Facts for 7, 8 and 9 · Focus: Facts 4, 6, 7, 8, 9

🎯 Objective: Bristol proves the week: mixed 4s, 6s, 7s, 8s and 9s facts, turn-around facts to pick the easier trick, missing factors (8 × ? = 56) by starting from the 5s and moving up or down, spotting wrong facts, and choosing multiply or add in word problems, including a sharing problem where the total is given.
Student goal: I can solve mixed facts, missing numbers and word problems.
Mastery to unlock the next lesson: 85

Materials

  • The Class lesson open on your screen (teacher code)
  • The 7s index cards from Tuesday plus new cards for 4s, 6s, 8s and 9s (about 20 cards)
  • About 60 beans and 9 small cups or plates
  • Whiteboard and marker
  • Her book, Amy and the Missing Puppy
  • Lesson 8 packet (2 pages) to send home

60-minute plan

0:00–0:03Hello
Class slide 1. Collect the Lesson 6 packet and Lesson 7 packet page 1. Check Tuesday's Show What You Know score.
0:03–0:10Book Talk
Class slide 2. How the girls work together and which character is most like her. Push for because with a reason from the book.
0:10–0:17Warm-up and card flip
Class slides 3–4. Three taps, then 3 minutes of fact cards: trick before answer, fast pile and slow pile.
0:17–0:24Flip it
Class slides 5–6. Build 6 rows of 9 beans, then turn the paper. Ask: did any beans go away? Which way is easier?
0:24–0:32Missing numbers
Class slides 7–8. Whiteboard: 8 × ? = 56, walk up from 8 × 5. She says too small or too big out loud on each tap.
0:32–0:40Total or one group?
Class slides 9–10. 24 bones, 8 in each bag: beans in cups to check. Then the treat bowls with real cups: 4 in each cup until all 28 are used.
0:40–0:44Facts that match
Class slide 11. She finds each answer with a trick before she taps.
0:44–0:52Word problems and fact detective
Class slides 12–13. She reads aloud: equal groups? total or groups? For the mistakes, she proves the right answer with a trick.
0:52–0:57Pick your trick and mixed problems
Class slides 14–15. She picks a trick for each fact and says why, then three mixed word problems.
0:57–1:00Slow pile and wrap-up
Class slide 16. Redo the slow-pile cards once. Read the weekend task list together and send the Lesson 8 packet home.

Watch for

  • Missing factor answered by subtracting (9 × ? = 63 → 54). Ask: 9 times what? Then start at 9 × 5.
  • Adding or subtracting the two numbers in a sharing problem (42 stickers, 7 on each page → 49 or 35). Ask: is 42 the total or one group? Then: 7 times what makes 42?
  • Turn-arounds she does not trust: she recomputes 8 × 7 after 7 × 8. Praise the check, then show they match.
  • 7 × 8 = 54 and 9 × 6 = 51 may come back. Make her prove the fact with the trick, not just change the answer.

Linked KCE lessons

  • Times Table Trail, Lesson 4: Multiplying by 6, 7, 8 and 9: Do the whole lesson. For 7s, say 5s part + 2s part. For 8s, double the 4s. For 9s, 10s take away one group. If it is locked, type 1111.
  • Times Table Trail, Lesson 5: Turn-Around Facts: Do the whole lesson. For each fact, say its turn-around out loud, like 6 × 9 and 9 × 6. If it is locked, type 1111.
  • Reading Foundations: Reading Star: Reading practice for this week. Do it on Saturday (it is on your task list). Show what you can do as a reader. Aim for 90 or more. If it is locked, type 1111.
  • Social Studies Week 6: Needs, Wants and Choices: Optional. Our Social Studies course, one week at a time: do this week after your other work. Answer from the evidence and aim for 85 or more so the next week opens. If it is locked, type 1111.

If a linked lesson is locked, the student types 1111 (you get a Formspree note), or you type 9110.
