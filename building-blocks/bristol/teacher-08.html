Teacher Plan · Lesson 8: Prove It: Mixed Facts

Thursday, November 5, 2026 · In person · 60 minutes · Week 3: Facts for 7, 8 and 9 · Focus: Facts 4, 6, 7, 8, 9

🎯 Objective: Bristol proves the week: mixed 4s, 6s, 7s, 8s and 9s facts, turn-around facts to pick the easier trick, missing factors (8 × ? = 56) by starting from the 5s and moving up or down, spotting wrong facts, and choosing multiply or add in word problems, including a sharing problem where the total is given.
Student goal: I can solve mixed facts, missing numbers and word problems.
Mastery to unlock the next lesson: 85

Materials

  • The 7s index cards from Tuesday plus new cards for 4s, 6s, 8s and 9s (about 20 cards)
  • About 60 beans and 9 small cups or plates
  • Whiteboard and marker
  • Her book, Amy and the Missing Puppy
  • Packet (2 pages) to send home

60-minute plan

0:00–0:07Book Talk
Slide 2. Ask how the girls work together and which character is most like her. Push for because.
0:07–0:14Warm-up and card flip
Slide 3. Then 3 minutes of fact cards: she names the trick before the answer. Make two piles: fast and slow. The slow pile comes back at the end.
0:14–0:22Flip it
Slides 4–5. Build 6 rows of 9 beans, then turn the paper. Ask: did any beans go away? Which way is easier for you?
0:22–0:32Missing numbers
Slides 6–7. On the whiteboard, write 8 × ? = 56 and walk up from 8 × 5. For each tap she says too small or too big out loud.
0:32–0:38Sort and build
Slides 8–9. For the treat bowls, she uses real beans and cups: put 4 in a cup until all 28 are used, then count cups.
0:38–0:47Word problems and fact detective
Slides 10–11. She reads each problem aloud and says: equal groups or not? Do I know the total? For the mistakes, she proves the right answer with a trick.
0:47–0:54Show What You Know
Slide 12, on her own. She needs 85 (7 of 8) to open Lesson 9.
0:54–1:00Slow pile and wrap-up
Redo the slow-pile cards once. Show the task list, including Times Table Trail Lesson 5. Everything is due before Monday's Zoom lesson.

Watch for

  • Missing factor answered by subtracting (9 × ? = 63 → 54). Ask: 9 times what? Then start at 9 × 5.
  • Adding or subtracting the two numbers in a sharing problem (42 stickers, 7 on each page → 49 or 35). Ask: is 42 the total or one group? Then: 7 times what makes 42?
  • Turn-arounds she does not trust: she recomputes 8 × 7 after 7 × 8. Praise the check, then show they match.
  • 7 × 8 = 54 and 9 × 6 = 51 may come back. Make her prove the fact with the trick, not just change the answer.

Linked KCE lessons

  • Times Table Trail, Lesson 5: Turn-Around Facts: Do the whole lesson. For each fact, say its turn-around out loud, like 6 × 9 and 9 × 6. If it is locked, type 1111.

If a linked lesson is locked, the student types 1111 (you get a Formspree note), or you type 9110.
