Teacher Plan · Lesson 1: Equal Groups or Not?

Tuesday, October 20, 2026 · In person · 60 minutes · Week 1: Equal Groups: Multiply or Add? · Focus: Multiply vs. add in word problems

🎯 Objective: Bristol can tell whether groups are equal and choose multiplication (equal groups) or addition (unequal groups), then solve. Her September diagnostic showed she computes correctly but picks the wrong operation in word problems.
Student goal: I can tell if groups are equal, and pick multiply or add.
Mastery to unlock the next lesson: 85

Materials

  • Counters or dried beans, about 40
  • 6 small cups or paper plates (to make groups)
  • Whiteboard and marker
  • 2 index cards (for her own stories; keep them for Thursday)
  • Her book, Amy and the Missing Puppy
  • Printed packet (2 pages) to send home

60-minute plan

0:00–0:07Book Talk
Class lesson slides 1–2. She tells you what happened last time she read: who was there, how Amy felt, and the clue that shows it. Write 3 of her words on the whiteboard. Listen; don't correct yet.
0:07–0:17Equal or not (hands-on)
Slides 3–6. Bean cups first: Set A (3 cups of 4) and Set B (4, 2, 5). Which set is fair? Then she makes Set B equal. On slide 6 she types equal or not equal and says how she knows.
0:17–0:30Equal groups → multiply
Slides 7–10. She circles each and every on the whiteboard. Groups × in each with Set A (3 × 4 = 12). She builds 3 groups of 5 and 5 groups of 3 to see which number is the groups. Then Theo's 4 bags of 6 with beans, then on screen.
0:30–0:40Not equal → add
Slides 11–13. 5 shells and 3 shells: add. Cat legs vs. cats and dogs: same numbers, different job (in all is not a multiply clue). Slide 13: six stories; she types multiply or add and says her clue first while you fill a T-chart on the whiteboard.
0:40–0:52Word problems and her own stories
Slides 14–16. Solve together (she reads aloud, says equal or not before typing). Index cards: one equal-groups story, one not-equal story with her book characters, built with beans. Detective steps in order.
0:52–0:57Quick check (alone)
Slide 17. Two problems, no help. If she misses one, redo it with bean cups before you wrap up.
0:57–1:00Wrap-up
Slide 18. Show her the task list on the course page and hand her the packet. Tonight she does the After-class lesson on her own; it ends with Show What You Know (85 opens Lesson 2).

Watch for

  • Adding the two numbers she sees no matter what (for example 4 + 6 for 4 tables with 6 cups). Make her build it with beans or draw it.
  • Mixing up 3 groups of 5 and 5 groups of 3 when building or drawing. Ask: which number is right before the word groups? That is how many cups.
  • Multiplying any time she sees in all (5 cats and 4 dogs is not 5 × 4). Ask: are there equal groups here?
  • Counting one by one instead of skip counting. Allow it today, but model skip counting each time.
