Teacher Plan · Lesson 1: Equal Groups or Not?

Tuesday, October 20, 2026 · In person · 60 minutes · Week 1: Equal Groups: Multiply or Add? · Focus: Multiply vs. add in word problems

🎯 Objective: Bristol can tell whether groups are equal and choose multiplication (equal groups) or addition (unequal groups), then solve. Her September diagnostic showed she computes correctly but picks the wrong operation in word problems.
Student goal: I can tell if groups are equal, and pick multiply or add.
Mastery to unlock the next lesson: 85

Materials

  • Counters or dried beans, about 40
  • 6 small cups or paper plates (to make groups)
  • Whiteboard and marker
  • Her book, Amy and the Missing Puppy
  • Printed packet (2 pages) to send home

60-minute plan

0:00–0:08Book Talk
Slide 2. She tells you what happened last time she read. Ask: who was there, how Amy felt, and what clue shows it. Don't correct yet; just listen.
0:08–0:20Equal or not (hands-on)
Before the slides: make 3 cups with 4 beans each and 3 cups with 4, 2 and 5. Ask: which set is equal? How do you know? Then do slides 3–5 together.
0:20–0:32Multiply equal groups
Slides 6–7. She builds 4 groups of 6 with beans, then on the screen. Have her say 4 groups of 6 is 24 and skip count 6, 12, 18, 24.
0:32–0:42Not equal → add
Slides 8–9. In the sort, she must say why before tapping. Push on the word each; it is the biggest clue.
0:42–0:50Word problems
Slide 10. She reads each problem aloud. Ask her to point to the numbers and say equal or not equal before she picks.
0:50–0:57Show What You Know
Slide 11, on her own. One try each. She needs 85 (7 of 8) to open Lesson 2.
0:57–1:00Wrap-up
Show her the task list on the course page. Packet pages 1 and 2 are due Thursday.

Watch for

  • Adding the two numbers she sees no matter what (for example 4 + 6 for 4 tables with 6 cups). Make her draw it.
  • Mixing up 3 groups of 5 and 5 groups of 3 when drawing. Ask: which number tells how many groups?
  • Counting one by one instead of skip counting. Allow it today, but model skip counting each time.
