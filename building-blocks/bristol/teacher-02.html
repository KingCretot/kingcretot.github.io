Teacher Plan · Lesson 2: Prove It: Find the Groups

Thursday, October 22, 2026 · In person · 60 minutes · Week 1: Equal Groups: Multiply or Add? · Focus: Multiply vs. add in word problems

🎯 Objective: Bristol proves the Week 1 skill on harder word problems: she finds the groups using clue words (each, every, per), rejects "in all" traps that are addition, writes the matching number sentence, reads arrays as equal groups in rows, and uses turn-around facts (3 × 4 = 4 × 3).
Student goal: I can find the groups in a word problem and write the number sentence.
Mastery to unlock the next lesson: 85

Materials

  • Counters or dried beans, about 40
  • 6 index cards: write the 6 sort cards from class slide 7 (keep Lesson 1's sort cards too)
  • Whiteboard and marker
  • A highlighter or colored pencil for clue words
  • A sheet of paper to turn the bean array (class slide 11)
  • Her book, Amy and the Missing Puppy
  • Printed packet (2 pages) to send home

60-minute plan

0:00–0:08Hello and Book Talk
Class slides 1–2. State today's goal. She names the big problem and retells 3 events with first, next, then. Push for complete sentences.
0:08–0:12Warm-up review
Class slide 3. She says equal or not equal and why, then types it. If she has not passed Lesson 1's check, redo the bean cups first.
0:12–0:19Clue words
Class slides 4–5. Write the kitten story on the whiteboard; she highlights every. Then 3 cages of 2 beans: she types groups, each and total.
0:19–0:29The "in all" trap and card sort
Class slides 6–8. Bunny story under the kitten story: no clue word, so add. Then the 6 index cards into Multiply and Add piles, saying the clue word for each, and the two trap items.
0:29–0:42Arrays and turn-arounds (beans)
Class slides 9–12. She builds 3 rows of 5 and skip counts. Then 3 rows of 4 on paper, turns the paper, counts 4 rows of 3. Ask: did the total change?
0:42–0:52Build, detective steps, number sentences
Class slides 13–15. Fish tanks, the 5 detective steps, then 3 stories read aloud; she writes each number sentence on the whiteboard before you click.
0:52–0:56Match the story
Class slide 16. She reads every choice aloud and names the groups and the number in each.
0:56–1:00Wrap-up
Class slide 17. Open the course page and read the task list. Tonight: the after-class lesson (it ends with Show What You Know). Practice, packet and reading are due before Monday's Zoom lesson.

Watch for

  • Multiplying any time she sees "in all" (for example 4 × 3 for 4 big bunnies and 3 baby bunnies). Ask: is there an each, every or per? Are the groups the same size?
  • Swapping groups and the number in each (writing 2 × 3 for 3 cages with 2 in each). Say: the groups come first. Point to the cages.
  • Counting an array's columns as rows. Have her slide a finger across ➡️ for each row.
  • Thinking a turn-around fact gives a new answer. Build both arrays with beans and count.
