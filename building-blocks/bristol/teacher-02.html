Teacher Plan · Lesson 2: Prove It: Find the Groups

Thursday, October 22, 2026 · In person · 60 minutes · Week 1: Equal Groups: Multiply or Add? · Focus: Multiply vs. add in word problems

🎯 Objective: Bristol proves the Week 1 skill on harder word problems: she finds the groups using clue words (each, every, per), rejects "in all" traps that are addition, writes the matching number sentence, reads arrays as equal groups in rows, and uses turn-around facts (3 × 4 = 4 × 3).
Student goal: I can find the groups in a word problem and write the number sentence.
Mastery to unlock the next lesson: 85

Materials

  • Counters or dried beans, about 40
  • Index cards: write the 6 sort cards from slide 10 (keep Lesson 1's sort cards too)
  • Whiteboard and marker
  • A highlighter or colored pencil for clue words
  • Her book, Amy and the Missing Puppy
  • Printed packet (2 pages) to send home

60-minute plan

0:00–0:07Book Talk
Slide 2. She names the big problem and retells 3 events in order with first, next, then. Push for complete sentences.
0:07–0:12Warm-up review
Slide 3 (recall). Before tapping, she says equal or not equal out loud and why. If she missed Lesson 1's check, redo the bean cups first.
0:12–0:22Clue words and the trap
Slides 4–6. Write both stories from slides 4 and 5 on the whiteboard. She highlights clue words. Ask: which number is the groups? Which is in each group?
0:22–0:32Arrays (hands-on)
She builds 3 rows of 5 beans and skip counts 5, 10, 15. Then she builds 3 rows of 4, turns the paper a quarter turn, and counts 4 rows of 3. Then slides 7–9. Ask: did the total change when we turned it?
0:32–0:43Sort, build, steps
Slides 10–12. In the sort, point out the pairs with the same numbers (3 cats/2 bows vs. 3 cats and 2 dogs). She must say the clue word before tapping.
0:43–0:47Match the story
Slide 13. She reads each choice aloud. Ask her to name the groups and the number in each.
0:47–0:55Show What You Know
Slide 14, on her own. One try each. She needs 85 (7 of 8) to open Lesson 3.
0:55–1:00Wrap-up
Show her the task list on the course page. Practice set, packet pages 1 and 2, and reading are due before Monday's Zoom lesson.

Watch for

  • Multiplying any time she sees "in all" (for example 4 × 3 for 4 big bunnies and 3 baby bunnies). Ask: is there an each, every or per? Are the groups the same size?
  • Swapping groups and the number in each (writing 2 × 3 for 3 cages with 2 in each). Say: the groups come first. Point to the cages.
  • Counting an array's columns as rows. Have her slide a finger across ➡️ for each row.
  • Thinking a turn-around fact gives a new answer. Build both arrays with beans and count.
