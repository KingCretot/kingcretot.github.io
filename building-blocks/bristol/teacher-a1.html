Teacher Plan · Bonus Lesson: Inside Amazon: How a Package Travels

Sunday, November 8, 2026 · Zoom · 60 minutes · Week 3: Inside Amazon: How a Package Travels · Focus: Bonus lesson

🎯 Objective: Bristol learns how an Amazon fulfillment center works (receive, stow, pick, pack, ship; robots carry pods, people pick) and how packages travel by truck, plane and ship, so she walks into Monday's visit as an expert. She practices her 4s, 6s, 7s, 8s and 9s and the multiply-or-add habit with Amazon stories. It is on Zoom: she says every answer and you type or tap it.
Student goal: I can tell the 5 steps a package takes inside Amazon and how it travels the world.
Score goal (this bonus lesson does not lock or unlock anything): 85

Materials

  • Zoom with screen share (open the Class lesson; teacher code 9110)
  • At her house (ask Tia to set out): about 40 beans or counters, paper, a pencil and crayons
  • A globe or world map you can hold up to the camera, if you have one
  • Your whiteboard for her guesses (slide 2)
  • Ask Tia to print the Field Notes packet: page 1 goes on the trip Monday; page 2 is for Tuesday

60-minute plan

0:00–0:04Hello
Class slide 1. She tells you about a package she remembers.
0:04–0:08Guess
Class slide 2. Write her guesses on the whiteboard.
0:08–0:14Word Power
Class slide 3. Five words; she uses one in a sentence about Monday.
0:14–0:22The 5 steps
Class slides 4–5. Act out each step on camera; she puts the steps in order from memory.
0:22–0:30Robots and people
Class slides 6–7. Robots carry pods; people pick. She sorts the jobs.
0:30–0:42Amazon math
Class slides 8–9. Beans for 4 × 6 and 3 × 8; one add-or-multiply story.
0:42–0:48Around the world
Class slide 10. Truck, plane, ship. Use the globe if you have one.
0:48–0:52Expert quiz
Class slide 11.
0:52–0:57Get ready
Class slide 12. She picks her question for the guide and writes it on Field Notes page 1.
0:57–1:00Plan
Class slide 13. Tonight the after-class lesson; Monday the Field Notes; Tuesday she shares.

Watch for

  • Mixing up pod (tall shelf) and tote (small tub).
  • Multiplying when the groups are not equal: ask "Is every group the same?"
  • 7s and 9s facts: use her 5-and-2 split from Lesson 7 (7 × 7 = 35 + 14).
