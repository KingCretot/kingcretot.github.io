Equal Groups or Not?

🎯 I can tell if groups are equal, and pick multiply or add.

Bristol learns what equal groups are. If the groups are equal, she multiplies. If they are not equal, she adds. Jonathan teaches the class lesson with beans and cups; she does the after-class lesson on her own and ends with Show What You Know.

Prove It: Find the Groups

🎯 I can find the groups in a word problem and write the number sentence.

Bristol proves this week's skill on harder word problems. She hunts for clue words (each, every, per), spots in all traps that are really addition, writes the number sentence, and sees that arrays are equal groups in rows and that 3 × 4 and 4 × 3 have the same total.

Fours: Double, Double

🎯 I can find 4 times a number by doubling two times.

Week 2 starts the facts Bristol does not know yet. She already knows her 2s, so today the 4s become double, double: 4 × 7 is 2 × 7 = 14, then 14 + 14 = 28. She checks with arrays and by skip counting by 4s.

Sixes: Fives and One More

🎯 I can find 6 times a number with my 5s and one more group.

Bristol knows her 5s, so the 6s become 5 groups plus one more group: 6 × 7 is 35 + 7 = 42. Then she mixes 4s (double, double) and 6s in pictures and word problems, and decides which trick fits each fact.

Tricks for 8s and 9s

🎯 I can use my 4s to find 8s, and my 10s to find 9s.

Bristol learns two shortcuts. ×8 = double the 4s (8 × 6: 4 × 6 = 24, double it to 48). ×9 = ×10 minus one group (9 × 7: 70 − 7 = 63). Both tricks start from facts she already knows, so she stops counting one by one.

Sevens: 5 and 2 More

🎯 I can split 7 into 5 and 2 to find my 7s facts.

Bristol learns ×7 = ×5 + ×2. She splits a 7-row array into 5 rows and 2 rows, finds each part with facts she knows, and adds (7 × 6 = 30 + 12 = 42). Today is hands-on: she builds and splits real arrays and sees a calendar as an array of 7s.

Word Problem Detective

🎯 I can ask 3 detective questions and pick add, subtract or multiply.

Bristol learns a 3-question detective routine for every word problem: What is happening? Are there equal groups? Which operation? She sorts add, subtract and multiply stories and matches each story to a picture. This goes after her biggest math habit: grabbing the numbers and picking the wrong operation.

Lesson 10 · Practice it

Price Tags and Totals

🎯 I can find the total cost when things have the same price.

Bristol uses the detective questions on money stories: same price for many things (multiply), different things bought together (add), and comparing costs (subtract). She skip counts $5 and $10 bills and learns the words price, total, factor, product and multiple. This covers the main Pathways Week 7 Math skills (skip counting $5 and $10, price × how many, the shop words). She retakes Pathways Week 7 Math after Thursday. This lesson also adds add and compare money stories.

Detective Challenge

🎯 I can solve mixed word problems and money problems on my own.
