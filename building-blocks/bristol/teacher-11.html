Teacher Plan · Lesson 11: Detective Challenge

Thursday, November 12, 2026 · In person · 60 minutes · Week 4: Word Problem Detective · Focus: Week 7 math to mastery

🎯 Objective: Bristol solves mixed one-step word problems on her own at the Pathways Week 7 level: price × how many, skip counting $5 and $10, how many she can buy (missing factor), comparing costs, adding two prices, spending, and the words price, total, factor, product and multiple. Watch for

  • Adding the price and the number of items (9 + 5 for 9 bags at $5). Ask: does every bag cost the same?
  • Giving the last skip-count number instead of the number of jumps on "how many can she buy". Have her lay down one bill per toy.
  • Regrouping slips in money subtraction ($50 − $27 as $33, $42 − $18 as $36). Ask her to check by adding back.
  • "In all" read as multiply when the amounts are different ($9 and $8 spent on two days).
  • Mixing up price and total. The price is for one; the total is for all.
  • Spelling the five words when she says them: price, total, factor, product, multiple. In Pathways she must type them correctly.
  • In the Pathways retake: typing $ in a money answer (marked wrong and costs the first-try points). Remind her before she starts: numbers only.
