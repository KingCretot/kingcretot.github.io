Teacher Plan · Lesson 10: Price Tags and Totals

Tuesday, November 10, 2026 · In person · 60 minutes · Week 4: Word Problem Detective · Focus: Week 7 math to mastery

🎯 Objective: Bristol finds totals in money stories: price × how many when every item costs the same, add for different items, subtract to compare costs or find what is left. She skip counts $5 and $10 bills and uses the words price, total, factor, product and multiple. Week 4 Day 2 (Practice it), aimed at the Pathways Week 7 Math retake.
Student goal: I can find the total cost when things have the same price.
Mastery to unlock the next lesson: 85

Materials

  • Play money: about 12 $5 bills, 10 $10 bills and 30 $1 bills
  • Index cards for price tags ($4 each, $5 each, $6 each, $10 each)
  • 5–6 small toys or objects to "sell"
  • 3 paper plates (for the build slide)
  • Whiteboard and marker
  • Her book, Amy and the Missing Puppy
  • Printed packet (2 pages) to send home
  • The Class lesson open on your laptop (course page, code 9110)

60-minute plan

0:00–0:03Hello + open the shop
Class slide 1. The shop is set up before she arrives. Write PRICE and HOW MANY on the whiteboard.
0:03–0:09Book Talk
Class slide 2. What happened since Monday, the clue she found, what Amy should buy and why.
0:09–0:13Recall
Class slide 3. Check Monday's After-Class score first. She says the 3 detective questions on the first item.
0:13–0:20Price + shop words
Class slides 4–5. She reads price tags three ways; says and spells price, total, factor, product, multiple while you write them.
0:20–0:28Skip count the money
Class slides 6–7. She lays one $5 bill per collar and counts aloud; then $10 bills. Bills (jumps) vs dollars (last number).
0:28–0:35Price × how many + build
Class slides 8–9. Frame: how many × price = total. She pays for 3 toys at $6 on 3 plates. Factors and product.
0:35–0:40Shop role-play
Class slide 10. You are the customer. She writes each number sentence on the whiteboard before giving the total.
0:40–0:47Not every money story is ✖️
Class slides 11–12. Act out same price, two different things, and compare; then she names the operation for six stories.
0:47–0:54Who spends more + shop problems
Class slides 13–14. You model finding both totals; then she solves three shop problems on the whiteboard.
0:54–0:57Quick check: shop words
Class slide 15. Price (spelled), product, multiples of 10.
0:57–1:00Wrap-up
Class slide 16. Show the task list: tonight the After-Class Lesson and the Lesson 9 Practice; Wednesday this Practice, the Lesson 9 Packet and page 1 of this Packet; 10 minutes of reading each night.

Watch for

  • Adding the price and the number (5 + 7 for 7 boxes at $5). Ask: does every box cost $5? Lay down one bill per box.
  • Giving the last skip-count number when the question asks how many bills or jumps. Have her touch each bill as she counts.
  • Multiplying two different prices ($6 and $8 → 48). Ask: is it many things at the same price, or two different things?
  • "How much more" answered by adding. Ask: are we putting together or comparing?
  • Mixing up factor and product. Factors go in, the product comes out.
  • Facts: 8 × 6 as 42, 4 × 8 as 36. Name the strategy (double the 4s; double, double).
