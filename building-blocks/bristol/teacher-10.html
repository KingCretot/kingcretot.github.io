Teacher Plan · Lesson 10: Price Tags and Totals

Tuesday, November 10, 2026 · In person · 60 minutes · Week 4: Word Problem Detective · Focus: Week 7 math to mastery

🎯 Objective: Bristol finds totals in money stories: price × how many when every item costs the same, add for different items, subtract to compare costs or find what is left. She skip counts $5 and $10 bills and uses the words price, total, factor, product and multiple. Week 4 Day 2 (Practice it), aimed at the Pathways Week 7 Math retake.
Student goal: I can find the total cost when things have the same price.
Mastery to unlock the next lesson: 85

Materials

  • Play money: about 12 $5 bills, 10 $10 bills and 30 $1 bills
  • Index cards for price tags ($4 each, $5 each, $6 each, $10 each)
  • 5–6 small toys or objects to "sell"
  • 3 paper plates (for the build slide)
  • Whiteboard and marker (for you)
  • Her book, Amy and the Missing Puppy
  • Printed packet (2 pages) to send home

60-minute plan

0:00–0:07Book Talk
Slide 2. She tells what happened since Monday and the clue she found (Monday's task). Then: what should Amy buy for the puppy?
0:07–0:12Recall
Slide 3. She says the 3 detective questions out loud on the first item before tapping. Check last night's packet quickly: any wrong operation?
0:12–0:21Set up the shop + words
Slides 4–5. Put price-tag cards on the toys. She reads each tag three ways ($4 each, $4 per toy, $4 a toy). On the vocab cards she says and spells each word aloud. Pathways asks her to type these words.
0:21–0:30Skip count money
Slides 6–7. She "buys" 6 collars with $5 bills, laying one bill per collar and counting 5, 10, 15 … aloud. Repeat with $10 bills. On the last item she counts jumps, not the last number.
0:30–0:39Price × how many
Slides 8–9. She pays for 3 toys at $6 with $1 bills on 3 plates, then does the build slide. Ask: which numbers are the factors? Which is the product?
0:39–0:50Multiply, add or subtract
Slides 10–12. Role-play: you are the customer. Ask a mix: "I want both of these" (add), "How much more is this one?" (subtract), "I want 4 of these" (multiply). She names the operation before she solves.
0:50–0:57Show What You Know
Slide 13, on her own. One try each. She needs 85 (7 of 8) to open Lesson 11.
0:57–1:00Wrap-up
Show the task list and the Times Table Trail, Lesson 7 link on the course page. Packet page 1 Wednesday, page 2 before Thursday.

Watch for

  • Adding the price and the number (5 + 7 for 7 boxes at $5). Ask: does every box cost $5? Lay down one bill per box.
  • Giving the last skip-count number when the question asks how many bills or jumps. Have her touch each bill as she counts.
  • Multiplying two different prices ($6 and $8 → 48). Ask: is it many things at the same price, or two different things?
  • "How much more" answered by adding. Ask: are we putting together or comparing?
  • Mixing up factor and product. Factors go in, the product comes out.
  • Facts: 8 × 6 as 42, 4 × 8 as 36. Name the strategy (double the 4s; double, double).
