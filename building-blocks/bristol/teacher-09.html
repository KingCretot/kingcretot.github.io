Teacher Plan · Lesson 9: Word Problem Detective

Monday, November 9, 2026 · Zoom · 60 minutes · Week 4: Word Problem Detective · Focus: Week 7 math to mastery

🎯 Objective: Bristol uses a 3-question routine (What is happening? Equal groups or not? Which operation?) to choose add, subtract or multiply in one-step word problems, and matches each story to a picture. This is Week 4 Day 1 (Learn it) toward mastering Pathways Week 7 Math.
Student goal: I can ask 3 detective questions and pick add, subtract or multiply.
Mastery to unlock the next lesson: 85

Materials

  • Zoom with screen share (open the Class lesson from the course page, code 9110)
  • Zoom whiteboard (or paper and marker to hold up to the camera) for you to draw her stories
  • At her home: about 20 beans, coins or blocks, a sheet of paper and a pencil
  • Her book, Amy and the Missing Puppy
  • Packet (2 pages) for Tia to print this week (it is due Thursday, on Tuesday's task list)

60-minute plan

0:00–0:03Hello + set up
Class slide 1. Share your screen. Check she has beans, paper, pencil and her book.
0:03–0:09Book Talk
Class slide 2. Where she stopped, a real clue from the text, what she would do as Amy.
0:09–0:13Recall
Class slide 3. She names the strategy before each answer (double the 4s; 10 groups take away one; equal shelves → multiply).
0:13–0:17Don't grab the numbers
Class slide 4. She builds 4 piles of 6 beans and proves 4 + 6 = 10 is wrong.
0:17–0:23The 3 questions + 3 kinds of stories
Class slides 5–6. She says the 3 questions from memory, then acts out join, take away and equal groups with beans on camera.
0:23–0:27Hands on: build the nests
Class slide 7. Beans first (5 piles of 3), then she tells you the numbers for the screen.
0:27–0:33Sort
Class slide 8. She reads each card aloud and says what is happening BEFORE choosing a box.
0:33–0:39I do, we do
Class slides 9–10. You model 7 dogs × 4 treats on the whiteboard; then she leads the 6 + 9 cats story, answering each question before you reveal it.
0:39–0:43What would you draw?
Class slide 11. She describes her picture before looking at the choices.
0:43–0:48Trap words + quick check
Class slides 12–13. "In all" can be multiply; "how many more" is subtract.
0:48–0:54Detective steps + tell me a story
Class slides 14–15. She orders the steps, then makes up stories for 6 × 4, 6 + 4 and 10 − 4 while you draw them.
0:54–0:58Guided practice
Class slide 16. She answers the 3 questions out loud on each story before giving the answer.
0:58–1:00Wrap-up
Class slide 17. She says the 3 questions from memory. Show tonight's task list: the After-Class Lesson (it ends with Show What You Know) and 10 minutes of reading. Nothing else tonight.

Watch for

  • Grabbing the two numbers and adding (14 + 8 for "how many more"). Ask: "What is happening? Is something joining or are we comparing?"
  • Thinking "in all" always means add. Point to each/every and ask: are the groups equal?
  • Subtracting in the wrong order (8 − 14). Ask: which amount is bigger? Start with the bigger one.
  • Swapping groups and each (6 hutches of 3 for 3 hutches of 6). Ask: what are the groups? How many are in each?
  • Facts 6–9 from Weeks 2–3 (54 for 8 × 7, or 45 for 9 × 6). Name the strategy: double the 4s, or 10 groups take away one.
