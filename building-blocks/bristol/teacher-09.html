Teacher Plan · Lesson 9: Word Problem Detective

Monday, November 9, 2026 · Zoom · 60 minutes · Week 4: Word Problem Detective · Focus: Week 7 math to mastery

🎯 Objective: Bristol uses a 3-question routine (What is happening? Equal groups or not? Which operation?) to choose add, subtract or multiply in one-step word problems, and matches each story to a picture. This is Week 4 Day 1 (Learn it) toward mastering Pathways Week 7 Math.
Student goal: I can ask 3 detective questions and pick add, subtract or multiply.
Mastery to unlock the next lesson: 85

Materials

  • Zoom with screen share
  • Zoom whiteboard (or paper and marker to hold up to the camera) for you to draw her stories
  • Her book, Amy and the Missing Puppy
  • 10–20 small objects at her home (beans, coins or blocks) for acting out stories
  • Packet (2 pages) for Tia to print for tonight

60-minute plan

0:00–0:07Book Talk
Slide 2. Ask where she stopped and what clues the girls have found. Link it: "Today you find clues in math stories."
0:07–0:12Recall
Slide 3. Before each tap she says the strategy out loud (double the 4s; 10 groups take away one; equal shelves → multiply).
0:12–0:21The 3 questions
Slides 4–5. She repeats the 3 questions until she can say them without looking. Have her act out each kind of story with her beans on camera: join, take away, equal groups.
0:21–0:28Sort
Slide 6. She reads each card aloud and answers "What is happening?" in her own words BEFORE she taps.
0:28–0:36Watch me + pictures
Slides 7–8. On the Zoom whiteboard, draw exactly what she tells you (7 circles, 4 dots each). If she says "7 plus 4", draw 7 and 4 and ask: does that show every dog getting treats?
0:36–0:42Trap words + order
Slides 9–10. Stress: "in all" can be multiply; "how many more" is subtract. She says each detective step as she taps it.
0:42–0:50Tell me a story + solve
Slide 11: she makes up a story for each fact and you draw it. Slide 12: she reads aloud and answers the 3 questions before tapping.
0:50–0:57Show What You Know
Slide 13, on her own. One try each. She needs 85 (7 of 8) to open Lesson 10.
0:57–1:00Wrap-up
Show the task list on the course page. Tonight: Practice and both packet pages (they are short). Ask Tia to send the packet with her on Tuesday.

Watch for

  • Grabbing the two numbers and adding (14 + 8 for "how many more"). Ask: "What is happening? Is something joining or are we comparing?"
  • Thinking "in all" always means add. Point to each/every and ask: are the groups equal?
  • Subtracting in the wrong order (8 − 14). Ask: which amount is bigger? Start with the bigger one.
  • Swapping groups and each (6 hutches of 3 for 3 hutches of 6). Ask: what are the groups? How many are in each?
  • Facts 6–9 from Weeks 2–3 (54 for 8 × 7, or 45 for 9 × 6). Name the strategy: double the 4s, or 10 groups take away one.
