Teacher Plan · Lesson 5: Prove It: 4s and 6s

Thursday, October 29, 2026 · In person · 60 minutes · Week 2: Facts for 4 and 6 · Focus: Facts 4, 6, 7, 8, 9

🎯 Objective: Bristol proves the Week 2 facts: mixed 4s (double, double) and 6s (5s + one more group), including turn-arounds; finds a missing factor (4 × ? = 24) by counting jumps or by guess, check and fix; and chooses multiply or add when two stories use the same numbers.
Student goal: I can solve 4s and 6s facts, find a missing number, and pick the right operation.
Mastery to unlock the next lesson: 85

Materials

  • About 50 counters or dried beans
  • 6 small cups or paper plates
  • Index cards: write 8 facts (4 × 6, 6 × 6, 6 × 8, 4 × 9, 6 × 4, 8 × 6, 4 × 7, 6 × 7) for a quick flash round
  • Whiteboard and marker
  • Her book, Amy and the Missing Puppy
  • Printed packet (2 pages) to send home

60-minute plan

0:00–0:08Hello and Book Talk
Class slides 1–2. She retells in order (her strength), then the harder prompts: most important clue, and how Amy has changed.
0:08–0:14Flash round and Remember
Class slides 3–4. Index cards: trick, then answer. Keep an "again" pile. Then the pick-the-trick review.
0:14–0:26Hiding numbers (hands-on)
Class slides 5–8. Number line for 4 × ? = 24 (count the jumps). 30 beans into cups of 6 (5 cups). Guess, check and fix for 6 × ? = 42, saying "too small" or "too big". Then three on her own.
0:26–0:31Mixed facts
Class slide 9 (sort). 4 × 9 and 6 × 6 are both 36: the answer alone does not tell you the fact.
0:31–0:42Same numbers, different job
Class slides 10–12. Build Story A (cups) and Story B (two piles). Word problems: she points to each/every or says it is missing. She builds 6 bowls of 8.
0:42–0:48Story hiding number and Fix it
Class slides 13–14. Act out 28 stickers, 4 per page, with beans. She explains Theo's and Mia's mistakes in her own words.
0:48–0:56Prove it on the board
Class slide 15. Hiding numbers and a multiply/add pair with the same numbers, written on the whiteboard before you click.
0:56–1:00Wrap-up
Class slide 16. Repeat the "again" flash cards. Read the weekend task list together, including Times Table Trail Lesson 3.

Watch for

  • Answering with the product instead of the hiding number (4 × ? = 24 → "24"). Ask: "How many jumps?"
  • Subtracting to find a hiding number (32 − 4 = 28). Have her check: does 4 × 28 make 32?
  • Multiplying any 4 and 6 she sees (Amy's 6 toys and Ellie's 4 toys = 24). Make her find the word each or every first.
  • The story hiding-number items on the check (for example 24 treats, 4 in each bag) need the hiding number, not the total. If she adds or subtracts, act it out with beans and cups.
