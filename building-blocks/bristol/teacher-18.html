Teacher Plan · Lesson 18: Spelling Team Champion

Tuesday, January 5, 2027 · In person · 60 minutes · Week 8: Welcome Back: Finish Spelling Teams: ea and oo, Then Super Sentences · Focus: Spelling patterns (ea, oo) (finish) + welcome-back review + Complete sentences, capitals, end marks

🎯 Objective: Bristol proves she can spell and read words with the ea team (long e, plus the few that say short e) and the oo team (the moon sound and the book sound), mixed together. She picks the right team by the middle sound, tells a team from a single short vowel, fixes misspelled words (red → read, bock → book, skool → school), and writes words from dictation. Week 7 Day 3 (Prove it). In person: she taps and types her own answers.
Student goal: I can spell words with ea and oo, and pick the right team.
Mastery to unlock the next lesson: 85

Materials

  • The Class lesson open on your laptop or tablet (course page, code 9110); she taps and types her own answers
  • The 10 oo cards from Lesson 17 (ask her to bring them, or make a set), plus 8 new index cards, face down: beach, read, dream, clean, school, food, book, look
  • Whiteboard and marker, plus paper and pencil
  • Her book
  • Printed packet (2 pages) to send home. Tell Tia about the spelling test on page 2 (it is on Sunday's line of the task list).

Watch for

  • Mixing the teams (meen or mean for moon, toom for team). Ask: "Long e, or the moon sound?"
  • One letter where a team is needed (bock, mon, bech, clen). Ask: "Is it a short sound, like bed or hot? If not, use a team."
  • red for read and bock for book: her own mistakes. Praise every time she gets them right.
  • skool or scool for school. It is a word to remember: s-c-h-o-o-l.
  • Reading head, bread or feather with long e. Remind her: try long e first; if it does not make sense, try short e.
