Teacher Plan · Lesson 18: Spelling Team Champion

Monday, January 4, 2027 · Zoom · 60 minutes · Week 8: Welcome Back: Finish Spelling Teams: ea and oo, Then Super Sentences · Focus: Spelling patterns (ea, oo) (finish) + welcome-back review + Complete sentences, capitals, end marks

🎯 Objective: Bristol proves she can spell and read words with the ea team (long e, plus the few that say short e) and the oo team (the moon sound and the book sound), mixed together. She picks the right team by the middle sound, tells a team from a single short vowel, fixes misspelled words (red → read, bock → book, skool → school), and writes words from dictation. Week 7 Day 3 (Prove it). Her first lesson after the winter break, on Zoom: it opens with a short welcome back (her Holiday Gazette pages and a quick remember check of ea and oo).
Student goal: I can spell words with ea and oo, and pick the right team.
Mastery to unlock the next lesson: 85

Materials

  • Zoom with screen share (open the Class lesson from the course page, code 9110)
  • At her home (ask Tia to set these out before the lesson): the 10 oo cards from Lesson 17 (or a set Tia makes), plus 8 new index cards, face down: beach, read, dream, clean, school, food, book, look
  • At her home: paper or a small whiteboard and a marker; she holds her writing up to the camera
  • Her Holiday Gazette pages (River's Diary and her Tray Math), next to the computer
  • Her book
  • Packet (2 pages) for Tia to print this week. Tell Tia about the spelling test on page 2 (it is on Wednesday's line of the task list).

Watch for

  • Mixing the teams (meen or mean for moon, toom for team). Ask: "Long e, or the moon sound?"
  • One letter where a team is needed (bock, mon, bech, clen). Ask: "Is it a short sound, like bed or hot? If not, use a team."
  • red for read and bock for book: her own mistakes. Praise every time she gets them right.
  • skool or scool for school. It is a word to remember: s-c-h-o-o-l.
  • Reading head, bread or feather with long e. Remind her: try long e first; if it does not make sense, try short e.
