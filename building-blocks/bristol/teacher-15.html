Teacher Plan · Lesson 15: Contraction Champion

Thursday, December 10, 2026 · In person · 60 minutes · Week 6: Contractions · Focus: Contractions

🎯 Objective: Bristol proves she can make and take apart every contraction from this week, mixed: the not family, the am/are/is/will/have families, and the odd ones won't (will not) and let's (let us). She chooses the contraction that makes sense in a sentence, fixes contraction mistakes, and writes her own sentences with contractions. Week 6 Day 3 (Prove it).
Student goal: I can make, fix and use contractions, even won't and let's.
Mastery to unlock the next lesson: 85

Watch for

  • won't mixed up with want, or written as wont or wo'nt. won't = will not; it ends with n't.
  • lets for let's. Say the long way: "let us go" → Let's go.
  • Picking a contraction that does not fit ("The kids isn't ready"). Have her say the sentence the long way.
  • were for we're and your for you're.
  • Apostrophes missing in her own sentences (cant, Ill). In the packet, check every contraction she writes.
  • Typed answers ignore apostrophes. The apostrophe spot is checked by the multiple-choice items, the sort and the packet.
