Teacher Plan · Lesson 8: Pick the Smartest Method

Thursday, November 5, 2026 · In person · 60 minutes · Week 3: The Area Model: Stop Adding, Start Multiplying · Focus: Area model / choosing the efficient method

🎯 Objective: Bailey chooses the optimal method for a multiplication problem (basic fact, break apart in her head, or area model on paper), finds and fixes blunders in someone else's repeated adding or area model, and solves one- and two-step word problems. This is the Prove it lesson for the area model week.
Student goal: I can choose the best method for a multiplication problem and find the mistake in someone's work.
Mastery to unlock the next lesson: 90

Materials

  • Index cards: 8 problems for a hands-on tool sort (9 × 8, 7 × 7, 14 × 6, 60 × 8, 12 × 9, 57 × 6, 418 × 7, 32 × 16)
  • Three paper plates or sticky notes labeled 🧠 Fact, 🔧 Break apart, 📐 Area model
  • Red pencil for marking blunders
  • Whiteboard, marker and grid paper
  • Her book, How to Train Your Dragon
  • Tuesday's Lesson 7 packet to check together
  • Printed packet (2 pages)

60-minute plan

0:00–0:06Book Talk
Slide 2. Push her to back up her opinion about Hiccup's choice with a detail from the book.
0:06–0:12Word Power + packet check
Slide 3. Flip all 7 cards. Then check one four-box model from the Lesson 7 packet: did she count four partial products?
0:12–0:22Tool sort (hands-on)
Before slides 4–5: she sorts your 8 index cards onto the three plates and says why for each. Then solve one card from each plate. Then slides 4 and 5.
0:22–0:28Pick and solve
Slide 6. She names the tool out loud before she taps anything.
0:28–0:38Blunder hunt
Slides 7–8. Before slide 8, write 53 × 8 = 40 + 24 = 64 on the whiteboard (fixed: 400 + 24 = 424) and let her find the blunder with the red pencil. She explains each blunder in her own words: lost zero, wrong fact, missing box, lost count.
0:38–0:46Recall + harder problems
Slides 9–10. The two-step problems: she writes the plan (multiply, then subtract or compare) before computing.
0:46–0:48Word Power
Slide 11, quick and out loud.
0:48–0:57Show What You Know
Slide 12 on her own, scratch paper allowed. One try each. She needs 90 (9 of 10) to open Lesson 9 (division).
0:57–1:00Wrap-up
Show the task list, including Multiplication Mountain Lesson 1 for the weekend. Celebrate the week: she can now do 75 × 8 without adding 8 seventy-five times.

Watch for

  • Choosing the area model for everything, even 9 × 7. Say: the optimal tool is the fastest one that still gets it right.
  • Defaulting back to adding over and over when a number feels big. Ask: how many boxes would this need? Then draw them.
  • Saying "there is no blunder" without checking. Make her redo one box and compare it to the estimate before deciding.
  • In two-step problems, stopping after the multiplication (192 oars instead of 42). Have her reread the question sentence and underline what it asks.
  • "I can't do it" on 29 × 16. Point to the four boxes: each one is a fact she knows.
