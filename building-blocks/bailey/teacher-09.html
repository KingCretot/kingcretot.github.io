Teacher Plan · Lesson 9: Division Undoes Multiplication

Monday, November 9, 2026 · Zoom · 60 minutes · Week 4: Division Undoes Multiplication · Focus: Division facts and fact families

🎯 Objective: Bailey understands division as the inverse of multiplication: she reads 56 ÷ 8 as "8 × ? = 56", tells a "how many in each" story from a "how many groups" story, and uses fact triangles for the 6–9 facts instead of adding the divisor over and over. She learns the words dividend, divisor and quotient.
Student goal: I can solve a division fact by asking the multiplication question, like 56 ÷ 8 means 8 × ? = 56.
Mastery to unlock the next lesson: 90

  • Zoom: share your screen with the Class lesson (teacher code 9110)
  • Whiteboard and marker (Jonathan), visible on camera
  • At Bailey's home (ask Tia to set out before the lesson): about 45 dried beans or counters and 6 cups or paper plates

Watch for

  • Adding the divisor over and over (8, 16, 24 …) to reach the dividend. Stop her kindly: "8 times what is 56? Which 8s fact do you know that is close?"
  • Mixing up divisor and quotient when naming parts. Point to the whiteboard line dividend ÷ divisor = quotient.
  • Writing the division backward (8 ÷ 56). Say: the top of the triangle, the biggest number, always comes first.
  • Using a fact that is one off (7 × 8 = 63). Have her check it with her ×7 shortcut: 5 × 8 + 2 × 8 = 40 + 16 = 56, so 7 × 8 = 56, not 63.
  • "I can't do it." Answer: "You already know the multiplication. Which fact is close?" Then wait.
