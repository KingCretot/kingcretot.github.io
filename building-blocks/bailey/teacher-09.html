Teacher Plan · Lesson 9: Division Undoes Multiplication

Monday, November 9, 2026 · Zoom · 60 minutes · Week 4: Division Undoes Multiplication · Focus: Division facts and fact families

🎯 Objective: Bailey understands division as the inverse of multiplication: she reads 56 ÷ 8 as "8 × ? = 56", tells a "how many in each" story from a "how many groups" story, and uses fact triangles for the 6–9 facts instead of adding the divisor over and over. She learns the words dividend, divisor and quotient.
Student goal: I can solve a division fact by asking the multiplication question, like 56 ÷ 8 means 8 × ? = 56.
Mastery to unlock the next lesson: 90

Materials

  • Zoom with the lesson on screen share
  • Whiteboard and marker (Jonathan)
  • At Bailey's home (ask Tia to set out): about 45 dried beans or counters and 6 cups or paper plates
  • 6 index cards and a pencil for making fact triangles
  • Her book, How to Train Your Dragon
  • Printed packet (2 pages)

60-minute plan

0:00–0:07Book Talk
Slides 1–2. She retells the last chapter in order, names Hiccup's current conflict and its type, and shares one new word with the clue she used. Praise the strategy, not just the answer.
0:07–0:15Word Power
Slide 3. She flips each card, reads it aloud and says her own sentence. On the whiteboard write dividend ÷ divisor = quotient under 56 ÷ 8 = 7 and leave it up all lesson.
0:15–0:24Two division questions (hands-on)
Slides 4–5. On camera she shares 24 beans onto 6 plates (how many in each), then makes plates of 6 (how many groups). Ask: same numbers, same answer, so what changed? (The question and the label.)
0:24–0:32Division asks a multiplication question
Slides 6–7. Every time she sees a division, she says the multiplication question out loud first. She makes three index-card fact triangles: 8-7-56, 7-9-63, 6-9-54, biggest number on top.
0:32–0:40Try it and build it
Slides 8–9. For the build, she also does it with beans: 42 beans onto 6 plates. Ask her to predict the answer with a fact before she moves a single bean.
0:40–0:48Parts, sort, review
Slides 10–12. In the sort she must say the multiplication fact before she taps a box. The review keeps the ×7 shortcut and the area model alive.
0:48–0:51Word work
Slide 13. She explains what clue in each sentence helped her.
0:51–0:58Show What You Know
Slide 14, on her own, camera on but no help. One try each. She needs 90 (9 of 10) to open Lesson 10.
0:58–1:00Wrap-up
Show the task list on the course page. Practice and both packet pages are due before Tuesday's lesson. She keeps her three triangle cards for Tuesday.

Watch for

  • Adding the divisor over and over (8, 16, 24 …) to reach the dividend. Stop her kindly: "8 times what is 56? Which 8s fact do you know that is close?"
  • Mixing up divisor and quotient when naming parts. Point to the whiteboard line dividend ÷ divisor = quotient.
  • Writing the division backward (8 ÷ 56). Say: the top of the triangle, the biggest number, always comes first.
  • Using a fact that is one off (7 × 8 = 63). Have her check it with her ×7 shortcut: 5 × 8 + 2 × 8 = 40 + 16 = 56, so 7 × 8 = 56, not 63.
  • "I can't do it." Answer: "You already know the multiplication. Which fact is close?" Then wait.
