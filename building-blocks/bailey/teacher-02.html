Teacher Plan · Lesson 2: Prove It: Every ×9 and ×6 Fact

Thursday, October 22, 2026 · In person · 60 minutes · Week 1: Fact Shortcuts: x9 and x6 · Focus: Facts 6–9 automatic + fact strategies

🎯 Objective: Bailey shows she owns every ×6 and ×9 fact to 10: she uses the turn-around (commutative) idea, finds missing factors (9 × ? = 63), spots and fixes flawed facts, and solves one- and two-step word problems. She learns 7 vocabulary words (multiple, equation, commutative, justify, flawed, reluctant, persevere) and reviews Tuesday's words.
Student goal: I can solve any ×9 or ×6 fact, find a missing factor, and fix a wrong fact.
Mastery to unlock the next lesson: 90

Materials

  • About 60 counters
  • A sheet of paper to rotate as an array (6 rows of 9)
  • Whiteboard and marker
  • Tuesday's 7 vocab index cards plus 7 new cards
  • Her book, How to Train Your Dragon
  • Printed packet (2 pages) to send home
  • 3 blank index cards for the Justify It slide

60-minute plan

0:00–0:03Hello + Lesson 1 check-in
Class slide 1. Collect Lesson 1 packet page 1 and look at her Lesson 1 after-class score. She names Tuesday's two shortcuts.
0:03–0:10Book Talk
Class slide 2. She retells in order and tells how Hiccup has changed, using ingenious or strategy.
0:10–0:14Recall
Class slide 3. She says each shortcut aloud before tapping. Rebuild any miss with counters.
0:14–0:22Word Power
Class slides 4–5. Teach the 7 words one at a time (say, clap, meaning, example, her sentence). Add 7 index cards to Tuesday's pile. Slide 5: she answers out loud first.
0:22–0:26All the facts
Class slide 6. Whiteboard quick-fire on ×6 and ×9 facts; she names the shortcut each time. Point out that 6s answers are even.
0:26–0:32Turn-around (hands-on)
Class slide 7. 6 rows of 9 counters on paper; turn the paper to show 9 rows of 6. She flips 7 × 9 and 8 × 6 out loud.
0:32–0:39Missing factors
Class slides 8–9. She says each one as a question ("9 times what is 63?") and verifies on the whiteboard.
0:39–0:46True or flawed
Class slides 10–11. She names her check before each tap and explains each mistake in her own words before tapping the fix.
0:46–0:53Word problems
Class slides 12–13. She says each step of the order aloud. Fish problem: whiteboard first, choices covered.
0:53–0:57Justify it (index cards)
Class slide 14. She writes 3 facts on index cards and justifies each one out loud with "because."
0:57–1:00Wrap-up
Class slide 15. Go through the task list on the course page: After-Class Lesson tonight; Practice Friday; this packet and Lesson 1 packet page 2 Saturday; Times Table Trail Lesson 4 Sunday; reading every day.

Watch for

  • Answering missing-factor problems with the total or a random fact (9 × ? = 63 → 54). Ask: "Say it as a question: 9 times what is 63?" Then make her verify.
  • Not flipping a fact she doesn't know (7 × 9). Ask: "Which factor has a shortcut?" and point to the 9.
  • Two-step word problems: she stops after the multiplication (54 fish) and forgets the second step. Ask: "Did anything else happen in the story?"
  • Using only the digit-sum check (accepting 9 × 3 = 72). Remind her: two checks catch more.
  • Choosing the slowest method or saying "I can't." Say: "You already proved you know 10s and 5s. Which shortcut fits?" Praise perseverance by name: "That's persevering."
