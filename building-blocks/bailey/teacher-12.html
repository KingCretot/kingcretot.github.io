Teacher Plan · Lesson 12: Welcome Back: What Did You Keep?

Thursday, December 3, 2026 · In person · 60 minutes · Week 5: Welcome Back Review · Focus: Retention / spaced review

🎯 Objective: Bailey presents her Gratitude Project poster, then shows what she retained from Weeks 1–4: the four 6–9 fact shortcuts (×9 = ×10 − one group, ×6 = ×5 + one group, ×8 = double, double, double, ×7 = ×5 + ×2), choosing a fact or the area model, the area model for 2-digit × 1-digit, division facts and fact families, and small remainders that must be smaller than the divisor. No new skill.
Student goal: I can show what I remember: fact shortcuts, the area model, division facts and leftovers.
Mastery to unlock the next lesson: 90

Watch for

  • Adding the same number over and over (9 + 9 + 9 …) when a fact is slow. Stop her kindly and ask: "Which shortcut fits a 9?"
  • On the ×9 shortcut, taking away the wrong group (6 × 9 = 60 − 9 = 51). Ask: "Ten groups of what? Then take away one group of that."
  • In the area model, treating the tens digit as ones (3 × 7 instead of 30 × 7). Point to the tens place and ask what the digit is worth.
  • Writing division facts backward (6 ÷ 48). Ask: "Can you share 6 beans among 48 plates?"
  • A remainder as big as or bigger than the divisor (60 ÷ 7 = 7 R 11). Ask: "Could another group fit?"
  • "I can't do it" after the break. Say: "You did this before the break. What is the first step?" Then praise the first step out loud before she does the next.
