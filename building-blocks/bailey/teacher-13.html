Teacher Plan · Lesson 13: Divide, Multiply, Subtract, Bring Down

Monday, December 7, 2026 · Zoom · 60 minutes · Week 6: Long Division, One Step at a Time · Focus: Long division, 1-digit divisors

🎯 Objective: Bailey learns the long division sequence (divide, multiply, subtract, bring down, repeat) and what each step means in place value (tens first, then ones; tens left over join the ones when you bring down). She estimates first by multiplying the divisor by tens, and solves 2-digit ÷ 1-digit problems with no remainder (84 ÷ 4, 96 ÷ 6, 72 ÷ 3), checking by multiplying.
Student goal: I can divide a 2-digit number by a 1-digit number with long division, one step at a time.
Mastery to unlock the next lesson: 90

Watch for

  • Writing the first quotient digit above the wrong place (above the ones). Ask: "Is that 2 tens or 2 ones? Where do tens go?"
  • Skipping the bring-down and dividing only the leftover (in 96 ÷ 6, dividing 3 ÷ 6). Point to the whiteboard: "After subtract comes…?"
  • A leftover that is as big as or bigger than the divisor (writing 1 in 92 ÷ 4 and leaving 5). Ask: "Is 5 smaller than 4? Could another 4 fit?"
  • Losing the place in the sequence. Have her touch the step on her paper and say it before writing.
  • Adding the divisor over and over to find how many fit (4, 8, 12 …). Ask for the fact instead: "4 times what is close to 12?"
  • "I can't do it." Do not explain the whole problem. Ask only: "What is the first step?" When she does it, praise that one step, then ask for the next.
