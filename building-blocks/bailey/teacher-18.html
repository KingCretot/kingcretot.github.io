Teacher Plan · Lesson 18: Prove It: Remainders and Checking

Thursday, December 17, 2026 · In person · 60 minutes · Week 7: Remainders and Checking · Focus: Long division, remainders + checking

🎯 Objective: Bailey proves Week 7 before the break: mixed long division with remainders (2- and 3-digit dividends, placeholder zeros, first digit too small), a two-part check (the expression quotient × divisor + remainder matches the dividend AND the remainder is smaller than the divisor), finding and fixing mistakes in worked problems, and deciding what a remainder means in a story (round up, drop it, or the remainder is the answer).

Watch for

  • Trusting a check that matches when the remainder is too big (141 R 7 for ÷ 6). Ask: "Part 2 of the check: is the remainder smaller than the divisor?"
  • Adding before multiplying in the check (154 × (6 + 1)). Say: "Multiply first, then add the remainder."
  • Omitting a placeholder zero when there is also a remainder (817 ÷ 4 = 24 R 1). Ask for an estimate: about 20 or about 200?
  • Dropping the 0 at the end of a quotient (165 ÷ 8 = 2 R 5 instead of 20 R 5). Ask: "Did you bring down every digit? What did you write after the last one?"
  • Giving "8 R 5 boats" in a story. Ask: "Is that practical? Read the question again."
  • "I can't do it" before the break, when she is tired. Ask only for the first step, praise it out loud, then the next.
