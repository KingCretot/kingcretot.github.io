Teacher Plan · Lesson 5: Pick the Fastest Path: 6s to 9s

Thursday, October 29, 2026 · In person · 60 minutes · Week 2: Fact Shortcuts: x8 and x7 · Focus: Facts 6–9 automatic + fact strategies

🎯 Objective: Bailey proves the 6–9 facts: she chooses the fastest of her four tools for any fact, checks answers with a second tool, finds missing factors, sees the four facts in a fact family, and solves mixed word problems (including one that is addition, not multiplication). New words: missing factor, operation, versatile, evaluate, demonstrate, inventive, victorious.
Student goal: I can pick the fastest shortcut for any 6, 7, 8 or 9 fact and prove my answer.
Mastery to unlock the next lesson: 90

Materials

  • 16 index cards, one 6–9 fact on each (for the tool sort)
  • About 60 counters for the 7 × 8 fact family array
  • Whiteboard and marker
  • Her book, How to Train Your Dragon
  • Her Lesson 3 packet and Lesson 4 packet page 1 to check
  • Lesson 5 packet printed to send home
  • Optional: a phone timer for the quick-fire

60-minute plan

0:00–0:05Hello + packet check
Class slide 1. Check the Lesson 3 packet and Lesson 4 packet page 1 (praise one thing, fix one thing). She names her four tools.
0:05–0:11Book Talk
Class slide 2. She compares two plans from the book and evaluates which was better.
0:11–0:14Recall
Class slide 3. She says each tool aloud before tapping.
0:14–0:22Word Power
Class slides 4–5. Teach the 7 words one at a time; add the index cards to the pile. She demonstrates 8 × 6 out loud.
0:22–0:30Toolbox card sort + quick-fire
Class slides 6–7. She sorts 16 fact cards into 4 tool piles and answers each; then the timed quick-fire, naming each tool.
0:30–0:35Pick the fastest path
Class slide 8. She evaluates each path out loud: does it work, how many steps?
0:35–0:42Fact families (counters)
Class slides 9–10. 7 rows of 8 counters: turn it for 8 × 7, share 56 into 8 rows for 56 ÷ 8. Preview only.
0:42–0:50Solve like a pro
Class slides 11–12. She says each step aloud and asks "equal groups or not?" before every problem.
0:50–0:54Add or multiply?
Class slide 13. She types the operation, then solves on the whiteboard.
0:54–0:58Defend your tool
Class slide 14. She picks index cards and defends a tool for each, demonstrating 6 × 9 two ways.
0:58–1:00Wrap-up
Class slide 15. Go through the task list and the Times Table Trail links. Hand her the Lesson 5 packet. Celebrate two weeks of fact tools.

Watch for

  • Going back to adding 7s or 8s over and over when a fact feels hard. Ask: "Which tool is that factor's tool?"
  • Picking the longest path for two-hard-factor facts (7 × 9 by 45 + 18 when 70 − 7 is faster). Praise that it works, then ask which has fewer steps.
  • Multiplying in an addition story (9 fish + 8 fish = 72). Have her say "equal groups or not?" before every problem.
  • Mixing up commutative (switch factors) with the division facts in a fact family. Use the triangle: the top number is the product.
  • "I can't do it." Hand her the index card and ask for just the tool name first, then just the first step.

Linked KCE lessons

  • Times Table Trail, Lesson 5: Turn-Around Facts: Do it on Friday or Saturday. Work through every part and aim for 90. It practices turn-around facts, like 7 × 8 = 8 × 7. If it is locked, type 1111.
  • Times Table Trail, Lesson 8: Mixed Review: Do it on Sunday, before Monday's lesson. It is a mixed review of the times tables. Aim for 90. If it is locked, type 1111.
