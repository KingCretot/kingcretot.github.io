Teacher Plan · Lesson 5: Pick the Fastest Path: 6s to 9s

Thursday, October 29, 2026 · In person · 60 minutes · Week 2: Fact Shortcuts: x8 and x7 · Focus: Facts 6–9 automatic + fact strategies

🎯 Objective: Bailey proves the 6–9 facts: she chooses the fastest of her four tools for any fact, checks answers with a second tool, finds missing factors, sees the four facts in a fact family, and solves mixed word problems (including one that is addition, not multiplication). New words: missing factor, operation, versatile, evaluate, demonstrate, inventive, victorious.
Student goal: I can pick the fastest shortcut for any 6, 7, 8 or 9 fact and prove my answer.
Mastery to unlock the next lesson: 90

Materials

  • 16 index cards, one 6–9 fact on each (for the tool sort game)
  • About 60 counters for the 7 × 8 fact family array
  • Whiteboard and marker
  • Her book, How to Train Your Dragon
  • Her Lesson 4 packet to check; Lesson 5 packet printed to send home
  • Optional: a 1-minute timer for a fact sprint

60-minute plan

0:00–0:07Book Talk + packet check
Slide 2. She compares two plans from the book and says which was better and why (this is today's skill: evaluate). Check her Lesson 4 packet and Times Table Trail Lesson 5 score.
0:07–0:12Recall
Slide 3. Ask her to say each tool aloud before she taps.
0:12–0:20Word Power
Slides 4–5. Flip all seven cards. She gives a book sentence for inventive and victorious, and demonstrates one fact out loud with a tool.
0:20–0:28Toolbox card sort (hands-on)
Slide 6, then the index cards: she sorts the 16 fact cards into 4 piles by tool, then answers each card. For cards with two hard factors (7 × 9, 6 × 8), she must say which tool is shorter and why.
0:28–0:34Pick the fastest path
Slide 7. Before tapping she evaluates each path out loud: Does it work? How many steps?
0:34–0:41Fact families
Slides 8–9. Build 7 rows of 8 counters. Show 7 × 8 and 8 × 7 by turning the array, then share 56 into 8 rows to show 56 ÷ 8 = 7. Keep it to a preview; division is Week 4.
0:41–0:48Solve like a pro
Slides 10–11. She reads each problem aloud. On the 9 + 8 fish problem, ask: "Equal groups or not?"
0:48–0:56Show What You Know
Slide 12 on her own. One try each. She needs 90 (9 of 10) to open Lesson 6.
0:56–1:00Wrap-up
Show the task list and the Times Table Trail Lesson 8 link. Celebrate two weeks of fact tools. Next week is the area model.

Watch for

  • Going back to adding 7s or 8s over and over when a fact feels hard. Ask: "Which tool is that factor's tool?"
  • Picking the longest path for two-hard-factor facts (7 × 9 by 45 + 18 when 70 − 7 is faster). Praise that it works, then ask which has fewer steps.
  • Multiplying in an addition story (9 fish + 8 fish = 72). Have her say "equal groups or not?" before every problem.
  • Mixing up commutative (switch factors) with the division facts in a fact family. Use the triangle: the top number is the product.
  • "I can't do it." Hand her the index card and ask for just the tool name first, then just the first step.
