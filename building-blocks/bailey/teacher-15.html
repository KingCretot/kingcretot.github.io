Teacher Plan · Lesson 15: Prove It: Long Division Detective

Thursday, December 10, 2026 · In person · 60 minutes · Week 6: Long Division, One Step at a Time · Focus: Long division, 1-digit divisors

🎯 Objective: Bailey proves the week: mixed long division with no remainder (2- and 3-digit dividends, placeholder zeros, first digit too small), deciding when a story is division (a total split into equal groups) or multiplication, finding and rectifying the mistake in a worked problem (subtraction slip, wrong fact, omitted zero), and checking every quotient by multiplying back with the area model.
Student goal: I can solve long division word problems, find the mistake in someone's work, and check my answer by multiplying.
Mastery to unlock the next lesson: 90

Watch for

  • Dividing in a story that needs multiplying (5 ships with 128 Vikings each). Ask: "Is a total being split, or are groups being put together?"
  • A bare number for a story answer. Ask: "57 what?" until she labels it.
  • Checking only the final answer when hunting for a mistake. Say: "The check tells you THAT. Now find WHERE: read me every subtraction."
  • Omitting the 0 at the end of a quotient (630 ÷ 3 = 21). Ask: "Did you bring down every digit? What happened to the last 0?"
  • In the area-model check, multiplying 1 × 6 instead of 100 × 6 for the hundreds box. Point to the place value.
  • "I can't do it" on a story. Say: "Read it to me. Is a total being split?" Then praise that first decision before the next step.
