Teacher Plan · Lesson 4: Break It Apart: The 7s

Tuesday, October 27, 2026 · In person · 60 minutes · Week 2: Fact Shortcuts: x8 and x7 · Focus: Facts 6–9 automatic + fact strategies

🎯 Objective: Bailey finds ×7 facts by decomposing 7 into 5 + 2, finding the two partial products, and adding (7 × 6 = 30 + 12 = 42). She learns 7 × 8 = 56 ("5, 6, 7, 8"), sees that her ×6, ×7 and ×9 shortcuts all use the distributive property, and learns seven new words (decompose, addend, distributive, fragment, reassemble, cunning, tactic).
Student goal: I can break 7 into 5 + 2 to find any ×7 fact.
Mastery to unlock the next lesson: 90

Materials

  • About 60 counters (or dried beans)
  • A pencil or straw to split arrays
  • Whiteboard and marker
  • 7 blank index cards for this lesson's words
  • Her book, How to Train Your Dragon
  • Lesson 3 packet and Lesson 4 packet, printed to send home

60-minute plan

0:00–0:03Hello + Lesson 3 score
Class slide 1. Look at her Lesson 3 after-class score together. She tells you what 7 is made of.
0:03–0:10Book Talk
Class slide 2. She breaks one of Hiccup's big jobs into steps (sets up today's idea) and names who has changed most.
0:10–0:14Recall
Class slide 3. She says each double or shortcut out loud before tapping.
0:14–0:23Word Power
Class slides 4–5. Teach the 7 words one at a time; she writes each on an index card. She names the addends in 30 + 12 = 42.
0:23–0:33Hands-on arrays
Class slides 6–7. Build 7 rows of 6 counters, pencil under row 5: 30 and 12. Then 7 × 8: 40 and 16. Chant "5, 6, 7, 8."
0:33–0:40Break it apart + build
Class slides 8–9. She writes each break-apart on the whiteboard before tapping; builds 7 piles of 7 and reassembles 35 + 14.
0:40–0:45Correct or broken
Class slide 10. She says which piece is wrong and what it should be before she types.
0:45–0:50Big idea
Class slides 11–12. Her ×6, ×7 and ×9 shortcuts are all the distributive property (×8 is doubling). Break 7 × 6 both ways; try 7 × 12 and 7 × 11.
0:50–0:54Sevens on Berk
Class slide 13. She reads aloud and names the equal groups.
0:54–0:58Teach it back
Class slide 14. She teaches 7 × 9 with counters and explains Snotlout's mistake.
0:58–1:00Wrap-up
Class slide 15. Go through the task list (heavy night: split Tuesday / Wednesday) and hand her the Lesson 3 and 4 packets.

Watch for

  • Forgetting to multiply the 2 (7 × 6 = 30 + 2). Point at the bottom 2 rows: "How many are in these two rows?"
  • Using 5 × 7 for the first part of 7 × 8 (35 + 16). Ask: "Which number did you break apart? Which one stays whole?"
  • Adding the partial products wrong (45 + 18). Have her use friendly tens: 45 + 15 = 60, then 3 more.
  • Going back to adding 7 over and over. Say: "That is the long road. Which tactic is shorter?"
  • "I can't do it." Cover the bottom 2 rows: "Can you do just the 5s part?" One piece at a time.
