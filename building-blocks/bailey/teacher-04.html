Teacher Plan · Lesson 4: Break It Apart: The 7s

Tuesday, October 27, 2026 · In person · 60 minutes · Week 2: Fact Shortcuts: x8 and x7 · Focus: Facts 6–9 automatic + fact strategies

🎯 Objective: Bailey finds ×7 facts by decomposing 7 into 5 + 2, finding the two partial products, and adding (7 × 6 = 30 + 12 = 42). She learns 7 × 8 = 56 ("5, 6, 7, 8"), sees that all her shortcuts use the distributive property, and learns seven new words (decompose, addend, distributive, fragment, reassemble, cunning, tactic).
Student goal: I can break 7 into 5 + 2 to find any ×7 fact.
Mastery to unlock the next lesson: 90

Materials

  • About 60 counters (or dried beans)
  • A pencil or straw to split arrays
  • Whiteboard and marker
  • 6 index cards with the sort facts (optional, to sort by hand first)
  • Her book, How to Train Your Dragon
  • Her Lesson 3 packet to check; Lesson 4 packet printed to send home

60-minute plan

0:00–0:07Book Talk + packet check
Slide 2. She retells, then plans how Hiccup could break a big job into steps (this sets up today's idea). Glance at her Lesson 3 packet; praise one thing, fix one thing.
0:07–0:12Recall
Slide 3. She says each double out loud. If she misses 9 × 3, ask "What is 10 groups? Now take one group away."
0:12–0:21Word Power
Slides 4–5. Flip all seven cards. She makes a book sentence for fragment, cunning and reassemble. Make her name the addends in 30 + 12 = 42, and explain how they come from 7 × 6.
0:21–0:33Hands-on arrays
Slides 6–7. Build 7 rows of 6 counters; she lays the pencil under row 5 and counts 30 and 12. Then build 7 × 8 and split it. Chant "5, 6, 7, 8" together.
0:33–0:43Break it apart, build, sort
Slides 8–10. For the sort, she must say why a card is broken before she taps (which piece was not multiplied?).
0:43–0:48Big idea + word problems
Slides 11–12. Connect ×6, ×7, ×9 and ×8 to one idea. Mention next week's 75 × 8. She reads each word problem aloud and names the groups.
0:48–0:56Show What You Know
Slide 13 on her own. One try each. She needs 90 (9 of 10) to open Lesson 5.
0:56–1:00Wrap-up
Show the task list and the Times Table Trail Lesson 5 link. Packet page 1 Wednesday, page 2 before Thursday.

Watch for

  • Forgetting to multiply the 2 (7 × 6 = 30 + 2). Point at the bottom 2 rows: "How many are in these two rows?"
  • Using 5 × 7 for the first part of 7 × 8 (35 + 16). Ask: "Which number did you break apart? Which one stays whole?"
  • Adding the partial products wrong (45 + 18). Have her use friendly tens: 45 + 15 = 60, then 3 more.
  • Going back to adding 7 over and over. Say: "That is the long road. Which tactic is shorter?"
  • "I can't do it." Cover the bottom 2 rows: "Can you do just the 5s part?" One piece at a time.
