Teacher Plan · Lesson 1: Smart Shortcuts for ×9 and ×6

Tuesday, October 20, 2026 · In person · 60 minutes · Week 1: Fact Shortcuts: x9 and x6 · Focus: Facts 6–9 automatic + fact strategies

🎯 Objective: Bailey replaces repeated adding with two fact strategies built on facts she already knows: ×9 = ×10 minus one group, and ×6 = ×5 plus one group. She uses the 9s digit-sum and tens-digit patterns to verify answers, and learns 7 vocabulary words (factor, product, strategy, efficient, tedious, ingenious, verify).
Student goal: I can use ×10 and ×5 facts to find ×9 and ×6 facts fast.
Mastery to unlock the next lesson: 90

Materials

  • About 80 dried beans or counters
  • 6 paper cups
  • Whiteboard and marker
  • 7 index cards, one vocabulary word on each
  • Her book, How to Train Your Dragon
  • Printed packet (2 pages) to send home

60-minute plan

0:00–0:02Hello and goal
Class slide 1. She reads the goal aloud. Fast means fewer steps, not rushing.
0:02–0:09Book Talk
Class slide 2. Retell in order, then Hiccup's conflict and how he solves problems with his brain. Link it to today: think smart, not long.
0:09–0:18Word Power (teach the 7 words)
Class slides 3–4. One index card at a time: she reads the word and meaning, you give the quick explanation from the slide notes, she says her own sentence. Then the word check (she says, then types). She keeps each card she uses correctly.
0:18–0:22Why adding is slow
Class slide 5. Race: she adds 7 nine times on the whiteboard while you say "70 minus 7 is 63." Ask which was efficient and which was tedious.
0:22–0:32×9 shortcut (beans)
Class slides 6–8. She builds 10 rows of 7 beans, slides one row away, then reveals the steps. Guided practice out loud (slide 7). Slide 8: the take-away-9 trap.
0:32–0:39Verify 9s
Class slides 9–10. Checks 1 and 2 first, then the finger check with her hands (they work for 9 × 2 up to 9 × 10). On slide 10 she says which check she used before typing correct or wrong; 9 × 7 = 54 is the tricky one.
0:39–0:48×6 shortcut (beans and cups)
Class slides 11–13. 5 rows of 8 plus one more row. 6 cups of 7 beans for the build. Guided practice, two steps out loud before each answer.
0:48–0:54Speed round and think smart
Class slides 14–15. Mixed 9s and 6s: she names the shortcut, you write her steps on the whiteboard. Then she picks the efficient strategy, fixes Fishlegs, and counts the additions in 7 + 7 + 7 + 7 + 7 + 7.
0:54–0:57Teach it back
Class slide 16. She explains both shortcuts to "Fishlegs" using factor, product, efficient and tedious. You only listen.
0:57–1:00Wrap-up
Class slide 17, then the course page task list. Tonight: the After-class lesson and its check (90 to open Lesson 2), then 15 minutes of reading. Wednesday: Practice, packet page 1 and reading. She takes the vocab cards home and brings them back Thursday.

Watch for

  • Falling back to repeated adding (her "add it 75 times" habit). Say: "Stop. What do you already know? 10 × 7." Then wait.
  • Taking away 9 instead of the other factor (9 × 7 = 70 − 9 = 61). Ask: "How many are in ONE group?" Point to one row of counters.
  • Adding 6 instead of the other factor for ×6 (6 × 8 = 40 + 6 = 46). Same fix: "What is in one row?"
  • Mixing the two shortcuts (adding for ×9, subtracting for ×6). Ask: "Is 9 more or less than 10? Is 6 more or less than 5?"
  • "I can't do it." Reframe: "You already know 10s and 5s. This is just one more step." Have her do the counters, then return to the slide.
