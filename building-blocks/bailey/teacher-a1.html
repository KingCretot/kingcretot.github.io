Teacher Plan · Bonus Lesson: Inside Amazon: From Click to Doorstep

Sunday, November 8, 2026 · Zoom · 60 minutes · Week 3: Inside Amazon: From Click to Doorstep · Focus: Bonus lesson

🎯 Objective: Bailey learns how an Amazon fulfillment center works (receive, stow, pick, pack, SLAM, ship), why robots make picking efficient, and how a global network of ships, planes and trucks moves packages, so she walks into Monday's visit as an expert with her own question for the guide. She practices Saturday's skill: estimate first, then a three-box or four-box area model. It is on Zoom: she says every answer and you type or tap it.
Student goal: I can explain how an order moves through a fulfillment center and around the world.
Score goal (this bonus lesson does not lock or unlock anything): 90

Materials

  • Zoom with screen share (open the Class lesson; teacher code 9110)
  • At her house (ask Tia to set out): paper and a pencil
  • A globe or world map you can hold up to the camera, if you have one
  • Your whiteboard for her predictions (slides 2 and 12)
  • Ask Tia to print the Field Notes packet: page 1 goes on the trip Monday; page 2 is for Tuesday

Watch for

  • Skipping the estimate: ask for it before she multiplies.
  • Area model: a missing box in 2-digit × 2-digit (24 × 12 needs all four: 200, 40, 40, 8).
  • Order of SLAM and ship: the label goes on before the package goes to the truck.
