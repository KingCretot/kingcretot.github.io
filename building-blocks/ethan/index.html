Malik's Mural: Multiplying Big Numbers

🎯 I can estimate first, then multiply 2-digit and 3-digit numbers by 2-digit numbers step by step and check my answer.

You connect the area model you already know to stacked partial products and the standard algorithm (18 × 13 = 180 + 54 = 234). You estimate first, line up the 0 in the tens row, regroup carefully, add the rows, and find the mistake in someone else's work, all with Malik's tile mural from Pathways Week 6.

Deja's Food Drive and the Class Pet Vote

🎯 I can divide with long division, check with multiplication, explain what a remainder means, and compare two viewpoints using evidence from the text.

You learn long division with Deja's 552 cans: Divide, Multiply, Subtract, Bring down, with 1-digit and 2-digit divisors and a multiples list. You write remainders, check every answer with multiplication (your Lesson 1 skill), and decide what a remainder means in a story. Then you read Malia's and Theo's pieces from The Class Pet Vote and compare, contrast and weigh their evidence (Pathways Week 7).

Wed, Nov 4 · 🏫 In person
Lesson 3 · Pathways Week 8

Sophia's Garden Plot and Ravi's Newsletter

🎯 I can add and subtract fractions with unlike denominators, write my answer in simplest form, and tell why an author wrote a piece.

You help Sophia plan her garden plot: you find a common denominator, rename fractions as equivalent fractions, add and subtract them, write answers in simplest form (or as a mixed number), and compare fractions like 1/4 and 1/6. You start with a warm-up on Lessons 1 and 2. Then you read Ravi's Newsletter and decide whether each piece was written to inform, persuade or entertain (Pathways Week 8).
