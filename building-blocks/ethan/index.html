Malik's Mural: Multiplying Big Numbers

🎯 I can estimate first, then multiply 2-digit and 3-digit numbers by 2-digit numbers step by step and check my answer.

Ethan connects the area model he already knows to the stacked partial products and the standard algorithm (18 × 13 = 180 + 54 = 234). He estimates first, lines up the 0 in the tens row, regroups carefully, adds the rows, and finds the mistake in someone else's work, all with Malik's tile mural from Pathways Week 6.

Lesson 2 · Pathways Week 7

Deja's Food Drive and the Class Pet Vote

🎯 I can divide with long division, check with multiplication, explain what a remainder means, and compare two viewpoints using evidence from the text.

You learn long division with Deja's 552 cans: Divide, Multiply, Subtract, Bring down, with 1-digit and 2-digit divisors and a multiples list. You write remainders, check every answer with multiplication (your Lesson 1 skill), and decide what a remainder means in a story. Then you read Malia's and Theo's pieces from The Class Pet Vote and compare, contrast and weigh their evidence (Pathways Week 7).
