The Houston Apartment Build and Building Up Houston

🎯 I can find equivalent fractions, simplify a fraction in one step with the GCF, convert between improper fractions and mixed numbers, and build an informative paragraph with a topic sentence, details, transitions and a concluding sentence.

You help Amara's crew at a Houston apartment build: you show that tile patterns like 3/8 and 9/24 are equivalent, simplify floor-plan fractions in one step with the GCF (30/42 = 5/7), and convert pipe and steel-bar lengths between improper fractions and mixed numbers (19/4 = 4 3/4). It is your last lesson of the year, and it hands you the Ferguson Family Winter Games.
