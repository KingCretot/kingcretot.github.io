Part 5: The Class Pet Vote

Malia: “A class hamster would teach us real responsibility. We'd take turns feeding it, cleaning its cage, and learning about how animals live. My cousin's class had a guinea pig, and everyone learned so much about caring for something besides themselves. It made the whole class feel closer, too.”
Theo: “I love animals, but a classroom isn't the best place for one. Someone has to remember to feed it every single day, including weekends and school breaks. A few kids in our class are allergic to fur, too. I think we should get a class fish tank instead. It's calming, and nobody's allergic to fish.”

14. Compare: How are Malia and Theo alike? Use the word both.
15. Contrast: How are their viewpoints different? Use the word while or but.
16. What is Theo's strongest evidence? Why is it strong?
17. Your viewpoint: How would you vote? Give one piece of evidence from the text, and name a compromise.
