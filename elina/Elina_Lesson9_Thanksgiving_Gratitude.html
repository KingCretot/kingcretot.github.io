Tutor notes · how this lesson runs (≈45 min)
Zoom setup: you share the screen and click everything; Elina only talks. Tap ⭐ +1 in the top bar each time she says a full English sentence (it's the score for the whole lesson). Each slide shows its target minutes (about 45 min in all). The 🔊 buttons use the browser voice.
Pattern of the day: "I'm thankful for ___ because ___." + "We eat ___." + "Do you like ___?" (thankful = reconnaissant(e); because = parce que)
French-speaker traps: "thankful of" → thankful for; "I like the turkey" (j'aime la dinde) → I like turkey (no "the" for food in general); "because" said "bé-cause" → bi-COZ; "Thanksgiving" with z/s → tongue out for th; "love", "brother", "pumpkin" said with a French « o » or « ou » → short uh, see the Sound spot.
Correcting: never "No." Repeat it back correctly ("Yes! You're thankful for your mom!") and let her say it again for the star.

Sound spot · short U2 min

😮 The Surprise Sound: U (uh)

Le son de la surprise : U (euh)
U (cup): open your mouth a little, relax, and say a short uh! 😮, like a little surprise. It's between « a » and « eu ». A (cap): open wide and smile: a! 😀U (cup) : ouvre un peu la bouche, détends-toi et dis un petit « eu » court, comme une petite surprise. C'est entre « a » et « eu ». A (cap) : ouvre grand et souris : a !
