Bibi's Day Is Upside Down! 🙃

La journée de Bibi est à l'envers !
🦇

Hi Elina! I have a problem. I'm a bat, so I sleep all day and I'm awake all night! I want to know: what does a girl do all day? You are the expert. Can you teach me?Salut Elina ! Je suis une chauve-souris : je dors le jour et je suis réveillée la nuit ! Qu'est-ce qu'une fille fait toute la journée ? Tu es l'experte. Tu peux m'apprendre ?

Today I can…tell you about my day — morning, afternoon, evening, and night!Aujourd'hui, je peux raconter ma journée — le matin, l'après-midi, le soir et la nuit !

By the end, you will say your whole day in English, all by yourself. 🌟

Tutor notes for this lesson
Goal pattern: "In the morning, I ___." + "First…, then…, after that…, finally…" + asking "What do you do in the ___?"
How to run it on Zoom: you share the screen and click; Elina talks. Every activity is a reason for her to say a full sentence. Tap 🗣️ + in the top bar every time she says one; it feeds the ceremony at the end.
French: hidden. Turn on 🇫🇷 Aide only to explain an instruction, then turn it off.
Watch for (French speakers): "I take my breakfast" → I eat breakfast; "I go to the school" → I go to school; "I brush the teeth" → I brush my teeth; "th" said as z/s.
Correcting: never "No, wrong." Repeat it back correctly with a smile ("Yes! You eat breakfast!") and let her say it again.

Tutor prompts
Keep it 3–5 minutes. Push for full sentences ("Good." → "I'm good!"). Follow-ups: "Why?", "What else?", "Who did you play with?"
If she says "I eat" for the past, recast: "Oh, you ate croissants!" Don't drill the past tense today; just model it.
She must ask you at least one question back. Answer it in 1–2 easy sentences she can copy.

Tutor prompts
Round 1: words showing; she says "I + action" for each. Round 2: press Hide the words and point; she says it from the picture only.
Speed round: you point fast, she answers fast. Count how many in 30 seconds.
Watch: eat breakfast (not "take"), brush my teeth (not "the"), go to school (no "the"), take a bath.

Sentence Machine · Is it true?

🎰 True or Silly?

Spin! Read the sentence. Is it true for you? If it's silly, fix it: "No! At night, I go to bed!"Lis la phrase. C'est vrai pour toi ? Si c'est bête, corrige-la !

🦷I brush my teeth.
Tutor prompts
She reads the sentence aloud, decides, and fixes the silly ones in a full sentence. That's the core skill of the lesson: making her own sentence from the pattern.
Then flip roles: she says a silly sentence about your day and you fix it ("No! In the morning, I go to school!"). Kids love catching the teacher.
First · Then · After that · Finally

🧩 Tell Me Your Morning

What do you do first? Tell me, and I'll put the pictures in your order.Qu'est-ce que tu fais d'abord ? Dis-le-moi et je place les images.

Tutor prompts
Step 1: she tells you one step at a time ("First, I wake up."). Click the pictures in the order she says.
Step 2: she retells all four in one go without stopping: "First, I wake up. Then, I brush my teeth. After that, I eat breakfast. Finally, I go to school." That's four sentences, so tap 🗣️ + four times!
Step 3 (if it's easy): do her evening the same way, out loud only.

Which one did I say? Point and say it back!Lequel j'ai dit ? Montre et répète !

Then I brush my teeth three times! 🦷🦷🦷
Tutor prompts
Say one word from a pair (don't click); she says which one. Then she says a word and you guess. Exaggerate the tongue on camera.
Finish with the twister three times: slow, normal, fast. Two minutes max; keep it light.
Game · Act it out

🎭 Guess What I'm Doing!

I act it out. You guess with a sentence: "You brush your teeth!" Then it's your turn to act!Je mime, tu devines avec une phrase. Puis c'est ton tour !

❓Mystery action
Tutor prompts
Round 1 (you act): click Pick a secret card while she looks away, peek, then act it on camera. She guesses: "You eat breakfast!" (practice you + your: "You brush your teeth").
Round 2 (she acts): she picks a card; you guess wrong on purpose so she has to correct you: "No! I take a bath!"
Questions version: she asks "Do you eat breakfast?" and you answer "Yes, I do / No, I don't."

Tutor prompts
She reads/asks each question; you answer as Bibi with silly bat answers ("In the morning, I go to bed!"). She reacts: "That's funny!" / "Me too!" / "Not me!"
Then swap: you ask her the same four questions; she answers about her real day. Bonus question: "What time…?" ("At 8 o'clock.") She knows numbers from Lesson 3.
