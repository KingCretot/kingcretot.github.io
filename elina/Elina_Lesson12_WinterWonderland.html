Every full sentence you say = ⭐. Can you get 40 stars today?Chaque phrase complète = une étoile ⭐. Tu peux gagner 40 étoiles aujourd'hui ?

Tutor notes · how this lesson runs (≈45 min)
Zoom setup: you share the screen and click everything; Elina only talks. Tap ⭐ +1 in the top bar each time she says a full English sentence (it's the score for the whole lesson). Each slide shows its target minutes.
Pattern of the day: "In winter, I can ___." + "I can ___, but I can't ___." + "Can you ___?" → "Yes, I can! / No, I can't."
French-speaker traps: "I can to ski" → I can ski (no "to" after can); "I can skiing" → I can go skiing; "I do sled" (faire de la luge) → I go sledding; "the winter" → in winter (no "the"); "skiing" said "skiinne" or "skiingue" → soft -ng (see the Sound spot). Build or make a snowman: both are correct.
Correcting: never "No." Repeat it back correctly ("Yes! You can ski!") and let her say it again for the star.

She chooses each next activity; you click what she says. Remind her: "I can" only once at the start ("In winter, I can build a snowman, go sledding, and bake cookies!"). Sound spot · NG2 min

🔔 The Bell Sound: NG

Le son de la cloche : NG
Open your mouth and hum through your nose, with the back of your tongue up: nnng… like a bell: ding-dong! 🔔 Don't say the G at the end: skiing, not "skiin-g"!Ouvre la bouche et fais vibrer le son dans ton nez, le fond de la langue en haut : nnng… comme une cloche : ding-dong ! On ne prononce pas le G à la fin : « skiing », pas « skiin-gue » !
