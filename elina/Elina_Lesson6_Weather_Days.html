Tutor notes · how this lesson runs (≈45 min)
Zoom setup: you share the screen and click everything; Elina only talks. Tap ⭐ +1 in the top bar each time she says a full English sentence (it's the score for the whole lesson). Each slide shows its target minutes.
Pattern of the day: "On Monday, it's sunny." + "What's the weather like?" + "What day is it today? Today is…"
French-speaker traps: "the Monday" → on Monday; "it makes sun / it makes cold" (il fait…) → it's sunny / it's cold; "the time" for le temps → the weather; days need a capital letter in English (Monday, not monday); say Wednesday = "WENZ-day", Tuesday = "TOOZ-day", Thursday with the tongue-out TH.
Correcting: never "No." Repeat it back correctly ("Yes! On Monday, it's sunny!") and let her say it again for the star.

Sound spot · AY3 min

🌧️ The Rainy-Day Sound: AY

Le son du jour de pluie : AY
Say « é », then slide to « i » with no stop: ay… rain, day! 🌧️ Two sounds in one! Not « dé », but day. For the short E (wet, pen), say only « è » and stop.Dis « é », puis glisse vers « i » sans t'arrêter : ay… rain, day ! Deux sons en un ! Pas « dé », mais day. Pour le E court (wet, pen), dis seulement « è » et arrête-toi.
