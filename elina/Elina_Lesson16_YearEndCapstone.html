Tutor notes · how this lesson runs (≈45 min)
Zoom setup: you share the screen and click everything; Elina only talks. Tap ⭐ +1 in the top bar each time she says a full English sentence (it's the score for the whole lesson). Each slide shows its target minutes.
What this lesson is: the review and celebration of the whole course (Lessons 1–15). Every game pulls from earlier lessons; the new pattern is "I can + verb" with an example from each lesson.
Pattern of the day: "I can ___." + "For example: ___." + "My favorite lesson was ___ because ___."
French-speaker traps: "I can to count" → I can count (no "to" after can); "I can speak English" is fine but push for details; "My favorite lesson was the Halloween" → the Halloween lesson or just Halloween; the hard sounds th, long ee, sh, and -teen (Sound spot).
Correcting: never "No." Repeat it back correctly and let her say it again for the star. Today, celebrate everything she can do.
