Tutor notes · how this lesson runs (≈45 min)
Zoom setup: you share the screen and click everything; Elina only talks. Tap ⭐ +1 in the top bar each time she says a full English sentence (it's the score for the whole lesson). Each slide shows its target minutes (about 45 min in all). The 🔊 buttons use the browser voice.
Pattern of the day: "It's ___, so I wear ___." (so = donc) + "I wear a coat / I wear boots" (no "a" with plurals) + "What do you wear when it's ___?"
French-speaker traps: "I carry a coat" (je porte) → I wear a coat; "It makes cold" (il fait froid) → It's cold; "a jean / a short" → jeans / shorts (always plural, no "a"); "baskets" → sneakers; "a pull" → a sweater; SH and CH both said "ch" → see the Sound spot (shoes / choose).
Correcting: never "No." Repeat it back correctly ("Yes! It's cold, so you wear a coat!") and let her say it again for the star.

Sound spot · SH / CH2 min

🤫 Shh or Achoo? SH or CH

« Chut » ou « atchoum » ? SH ou CH
SH (shoes) is soft and long, like "shh!" for quiet: shhh 🤫. CH (choose) is short, with a little T first, like a sneeze: a-CHOO! 🤧SH (shoes), c'est doux et long, comme « chut ! » en français : chhh. CH (choose), c'est court, avec un petit T devant, comme un éternuement : a-TCHOUM !

Listen. Which one did you hear? Then say both!Écoute. Lequel tu as entendu ? Puis dis les deux !

She chooses shorts and a shirt! 🩳👕Elle choisit un short et une chemise !
Tutor prompts
The purple word in each pair has the soft SH; the other has CH (= "tch"). Press Mystery word: it plays one word from a pair; she says which one. Then she says a word and you guess. Link to today: shorts, shoes, shirt. Finish with the twister: slow, normal, fast.
