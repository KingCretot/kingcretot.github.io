Tutor notes · how this lesson runs (≈45 min)
Zoom setup: you share the screen and click everything; Elina only talks. Tap ⭐ +1 in the top bar each time she says a full English sentence (it's the score for the whole lesson). Each slide shows its target minutes (about 45 min in all). The 🔊 buttons use the browser voice.
Pattern of the day: "My ___ hurts." / "My ___ hurt." (plural) + "I feel ___." + "When ___, I feel ___." + "How do you feel?"
French-speaker traps: "I have mal at the head" → My head hurts; "I have hunger / I have cold" → I'm hungry / I'm cold; "My eyes hurts" → My eyes hurt (plural: no -s); "I am worry" → I'm worried / I feel worried; "I am surprise" → I'm surprised; silent H ("'ead", "'and") → breathe the H (see the Sound spot).
Correcting: never "No." Repeat it back correctly ("Oh no! Your head hurts!") and let her say it again for the star.
